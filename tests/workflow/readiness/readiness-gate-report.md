## What this does not show

The gate is a *delegation* check, not a run. These probes prove the `readiness` segment is unblocked and that prompt drift re-blocks it; they do not produce the readiness success rate, which task 7.5 owns and which only accumulates from real runs on real items. No end-to-end rate is claimed for the pipeline, and five of its six segments remain uncertified.

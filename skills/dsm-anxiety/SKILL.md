# Anxiety Disorders

Share **excessive fear and anxiety with related behavioural disturbance**. The manual draws a
distinction worth keeping: **fear** is the response to imminent threat (autonomic surge, fight
or flight), **anxiety** is anticipation of future threat (vigilance, muscle tension, avoidance).
Panic is fear misfiring; GAD is anxiety.

Ordered **developmentally** — separation anxiety and selective mutism first (typically earliest
onset), GAD last.

DSM-5 moved **OCD** ([[dsm-ocd-related]]) and **PTSD/acute stress** ([[dsm-trauma-stressor]])
out of this chapter into their own, because factor and neurobiological evidence put them
adjacent rather than inside.

## The disorders

- **Separation anxiety disorder** — developmentally inappropriate fear of separation from
  attachment figures. **DSM-5 removed the before-age-18 onset requirement**, so adults can be
  diagnosed; duration ≥4 weeks in children, **≥6 months in adults**.
- **Selective mutism** — consistent failure to speak in specific social situations where
  speech is expected, ≥1 month (not limited to the first month of school). Moved into this
  chapter in DSM-5.
- **Specific phobia** — ≥6 months. **Specify** the stimulus: animal, natural environment,
  blood-injection-injury, situational, other. The blood-injection-injury type is the one with a
  vasovagal fainting response rather than tachycardia.
- **Social anxiety disorder** — fear of scrutiny and negative evaluation, ≥6 months. **Specify
  if performance only.** DSM-5 renamed it from "social phobia" and dropped the "generalized"
  specifier in favour of the performance-only one.
- **Panic disorder** — recurrent unexpected panic attacks plus ≥1 month of worry about further
  attacks or maladaptive change in behaviour. **The attacks must be unexpected**, at least
  initially.
- **Panic attack** is a **specifier**, not a disorder — applicable across the whole manual
  (to PTSD, substance use, depression). That is a DSM-5 change and it is widely missed.
- **Agoraphobia** — fear of ≥2 of five situations (public transport, open spaces, enclosed
  spaces, queues/crowds, being outside the home alone), ≥6 months. **DSM-5 made it an
  independent diagnosis** rather than a panic-disorder modifier, so a person can have both,
  coded separately.
- **Generalized anxiety disorder** — excessive worry about multiple domains, ≥6 months, more
  days than not, plus ≥3 of six associated symptoms (one in children).
- **Substance/medication-induced** and **due to another medical condition**.

## Differentials

- **Medical first**, because this chapter has the most mimics: hyperthyroidism,
  pheochromocytoma, arrhythmia, asthma/COPD, hypoglycaemia, vestibular disorders, mitral valve
  disease. A first panic attack in middle age deserves a workup, not a formulation.
- **Substance:** caffeine (a coded intoxication in [[dsm-substance-related]]), stimulants,
  cannabis, alcohol and sedative withdrawal, salbutamol, thyroid replacement.
- **GAD vs obsessions** — GAD worries are about real-life concerns and feel ego-syntonic;
  obsessions are intrusive, often implausible, and paired with compulsions
  ([[dsm-ocd-related]]).
- **Social anxiety vs autism** — avoidance from fear of evaluation vs social-communication
  deficit; they co-occur ([[dsm-neurodevelopmental]]).
- **Agoraphobia vs specific phobia, situational type** — number of situations and the nature of
  the fear (escape/help unavailable vs the situation itself).
- **PTSD avoidance** is trauma-cued ([[dsm-trauma-stressor]]).
- **Normal fear** — the six-month duration and the excessiveness judgment are what separate
  disorder from proportionate fear, and culture shapes both ([[dsm-5]]).

## Rails

Not a diagnostic instrument. **Never assign a diagnosis to the user or a third party** — explain
what a category means instead. Criteria require clinically significant distress or impairment
plus exclusion of substance/medication and other medical causes. If self-harm, suicidality,
abuse or acute crisis surfaces, drop the taxonomy and respond to the person: **988** (US/Canada
call or text), or local emergency services. Criteria text is copyrighted and not reproduced —
named only. Full rails in [[dsm-5]].

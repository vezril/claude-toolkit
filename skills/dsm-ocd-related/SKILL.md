# Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders

**New chapter in DSM-5**, split from the anxiety disorders. The grouping claim is that these
conditions share **obsessive preoccupation and repetitive behaviours**, with family-history,
neurocircuitry and treatment-response evidence binding them more tightly to each other than to
anxiety.

## The disorders

- **Obsessive-compulsive disorder** — obsessions (intrusive, unwanted thoughts/urges/images)
  and/or compulsions (repetitive behaviours or mental acts performed to reduce distress or
  prevent a feared outcome), time-consuming or impairing.
- **Body dysmorphic disorder** — preoccupation with perceived defects in appearance not
  observable or slight to others, with repetitive behaviours (mirror checking, comparing).
  **Specify if with muscle dysmorphia.** Moved here from the somatoform disorders.
- **Hoarding disorder** — **new in DSM-5**. Persistent difficulty discarding possessions due to
  perceived need and distress at parting, resulting in accumulation that congests living areas.
  **Specify if with excessive acquisition.** Previously treated as an OCPD criterion or an OCD
  symptom; separated because course, insight and treatment response differ.
- **Trichotillomania (hair-pulling disorder)** — recurrent pulling with repeated attempts to
  stop. Moved here from impulse-control disorders.
- **Excoriation (skin-picking) disorder** — **new in DSM-5**.
- **Substance/medication-induced** and **due to another medical condition** — the latter
  includes **PANDAS**-type presentations and basal ganglia lesions.

## The two specifiers that carry the most weight

- **Insight: good or fair / poor / absent or delusional.** This is the chapter's answer to a
  real problem — a person wholly convinced their compulsions are necessary, or that the defect
  is real, is still in this chapter, **not** in [[dsm-schizophrenia-spectrum]]. Absent insight
  is a specifier, not a reclassification.
- **Tic-related** (for OCD) — a lifetime history of a tic disorder. Marks a distinct subtype
  with different comorbidity, course and treatment response
  ([[dsm-neurodevelopmental]]).

## Differentials

- **Obsessions vs GAD worry** — obsessions are intrusive, ego-dystonic, often implausible, and
  paired with compulsions; worry is about plausible real-life concerns
  ([[dsm-anxiety]]).
- **Compulsions vs tics vs stereotypies** — compulsions are purposeful and aimed at preventing
  or reducing distress; tics are briefer, non-goal-directed, with a premonitory urge
  ([[dsm-neurodevelopmental]]).
- **Absent-insight OCD/BDD vs delusional disorder** — the insight specifier keeps it here.
  Genuine somatic delusions without the OCD/BDD structure go to
  [[dsm-schizophrenia-spectrum]].
- **BDD vs anorexia nervosa** — if the preoccupation is only about weight and body fat with
  restriction, it is an eating disorder ([[dsm-feeding-eating]]). BDD concerns other features
  or is not weight-driven; both can be present.
- **OCD vs obsessive-compulsive personality disorder** — OCPD is a pervasive trait pattern of
  perfectionism and control **without** true obsessions and compulsions. Different chapter,
  different thing, confusingly similar name ([[dsm-personality]]).
- **Hoarding vs normal collecting** — collecting is organised and non-distressing; hoarding
  congests space and function.
- **Body-focused repetitive behaviours vs self-injury** — intent differs; see
  [[dsm-other-conditions]] for nonsuicidal self-injury codes.

## Rails

Not a diagnostic instrument. **Never assign a diagnosis to the user or a third party** — explain
what a category means instead. Criteria require clinically significant distress or impairment
plus exclusion of substance/medication and other medical causes. If self-harm, suicidality,
abuse or acute crisis surfaces, drop the taxonomy and respond to the person: **988** (US/Canada
call or text), or local emergency services. Criteria text is copyrighted and not reproduced —
named only. Full rails in [[dsm-5]].

# Clan bosses: Demon Lord, Hydra, Chimera

## Clan Boss (Demon Lord)

**Basics.** Six difficulties, Easy through Ultra-Nightmare (UNM). The boss holds at most 10
debuffs. Keys respawn every 6 hours, for up to four free hits a day.

**Boss behaviour:**

- From turn 10, Gathering Fury ramps its damage.
- From turn 50 it ignores Block Damage and Unkillable and applies Block Revive to the champions
  it kills.
- It is immune to Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Decrease SPD and Turn Meter reduction, among
  others.
- UNM boss speed is 190.
- Poison caps at about 50K per tick on the top difficulties.

**Its skills:**

- **A1:** an unresistable 1-turn stun on one champion, scaled by max HP. Hence the dedicated
  "stun target".
- **The other two:** depend on affinity. Void poisons, Force decreases ATK, Magic decreases
  ACC, and Spirit decreases SPD.

**Affinity is a live conflict** `[CHECK IN-GAME]`. The wiki (2025-10) and HellHades (2026-10)
say each day starts as Void and switches below 50% HP. DeadwoodJedi gives per-day advice
("swap Ninja out on Force days"). **Ask the player for today's affinity.** Don't predict it.

HellHades' build priority: DEF over HP "99 of 100 times". Great Hall medals go to ACC first,
then DEF.

## Hydra

**Basics.** 3 keys a week, Normal to Nightmare. You field 6 champions against 4 of the 6
heads; the other two wait as substitutes. A decapitated head takes 200% more damage while
exposed. A regrown head carries 75% damage reduction. Devoured champions are lost unless freed
in time.

**Caps `[VERIFIED]`:**

- Max-HP damage: 10% of the head's max HP.
- HP Burn: 20,000 per tick.
- Reflect, Warmaster and Giant Slayer: 50,000 per attack each.

**Ask which heads are up this rotation**, fill the counter slots, then the damage slots:

| Head | Threat | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Torment | Fear, then hits every Feared champion; 90% poison reduction | Perfect Veil or regular cleansing |
| Decay | Heal Reduction; heals cut your max HP | Provoke |
| Blight | Stacked poisons and Leech | Block Buffs |
| Suffering | AoE Weaken; +50 RES to all heads; Provokes your highest-C.DMG champion | Block Buffs |
| Mischief | Steals buffs and turn meter; redirects attacks | A high-RES self-buffer "tank"; Hex |
| Wrath | AoE Decrease ATK; Vengeance at 15 hits | Block Buffs |

**The leading damage plan**, as of September 2025:

- **Titus Blackplume**, whose A2 scales on Shield size.
- **Wixwell** to grow the shield; Bloodshield accessories are the budget substitute.
- Three buff extenders: Krisk, Mad Hatter, Demytha or Lady Mikage.

Trunda remains the top single damage dealer. Shield-scaling teams have been nerfed twice
before, so present this as "current as of Sep 2025, check for changes".

**Top chests `[VERIFIED]`** (weekly damage): Normal >6.66M, Hard >20.4M, Brutal >29.4M,
Nightmare >36.6M. HellHades' "215 ACC on Normal" (head RES 140) is about 2.5 years old
`[CHECK IN-GAME]`; point to HellHades' stage tool.

## Chimera

**Basics.** The third clan boss (late 2024) and the only source of the Mythical **Embrys the
Anomaly**. 5 champions, 65 turns, 2 keys a week.

**The form cycle.** Every 5 turns the boss moves through U → R → U → L → U → V → U. U is the
Ultimate Form; R, L and V are the Ram, Lion and Viper forms. Each return to Ultimate wipes its
buffs and debuffs. Ram, Lion and Viper share one affinity per 28-day rotation, so ask which.

**Stats `[VERIFIED]`, Ayumilove.**

- At UNM the boss has 1.61B HP and 305 SPD.
- The Ultimate Form has 400 RES. Ram reaches 450 RES and 500 ACC.
- Use the RES + 25 rule for ACC per form, and RES above its ACC + 125 to resist it.
- UNM top chest: >112.5M.

**Trials.** Completed form Trials earn rewards and Chimera Clash points, but they also empower
the Ultimate Form. So a Chimera team is a toolkit:

| Form | Bring |
|---|---|
| Ram | Fear, Weaken, Decrease DEF; Block Debuffs or Intercept against its stuns |
| Lion | Shields, Increase DEF, Block Damage, counterattack or Ally Attack |
| Viper | Block Active Skills, a reviver, Decrease ACC |

The research found no named Chimera team comps. HellHades renders them from its optimiser.
Build from the roles above and the player's roster.

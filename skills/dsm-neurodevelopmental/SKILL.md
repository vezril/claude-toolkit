# Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Onset **in the developmental period**, typically before school entry, producing deficits in
personal, social, academic or occupational functioning. First chapter in the manual because the
ordering is developmental.

Characteristic feature: these co-occur heavily with each other. Autism with ADHD, ADHD with
specific learning disorder, intellectual developmental disorder with almost anything. DSM-5
**removed the DSM-IV prohibition** on diagnosing ADHD alongside autism, which was a substantive
change.

## What's in the chapter

- **Intellectual developmental disorder (intellectual disability)** — deficits in intellectual
  *and* adaptive functioning, with onset in development. **Severity is set by adaptive
  functioning, not IQ** — a DSM-5 change from the IQ-band approach, because adaptive
  functioning determines support needs. Conceptual, social and practical domains.
  Plus **global developmental delay** (under 5, severity not reliably assessable) and the
  unspecified variant.
- **Communication disorders** — language disorder, speech sound disorder, childhood-onset
  fluency disorder (stuttering), and **social (pragmatic) communication disorder**, which is
  the one to know: pragmatic deficits *without* the restricted/repetitive behaviours of autism.
- **Autism spectrum disorder** — one diagnosis replacing DSM-IV's autistic disorder, Asperger's,
  PDD-NOS and childhood disintegrative disorder. Two symptom domains (social communication;
  restricted/repetitive behaviours, which now explicitly include sensory reactivity), each
  rated by **support level** — requiring support / substantial / very substantial. Specifiers
  for accompanying intellectual and language impairment, known genetic or medical association,
  and catatonia.
- **ADHD** — inattention and/or hyperactivity-impulsivity. **Specify whether** combined
  (`F90.2`), predominantly inattentive (`F90.0`), predominantly hyperactive/impulsive
  (`F90.1`); severity mild/moderate/severe; *in partial remission*. DSM-5 moved onset to
  **before age 12** (from 7) and lowered the adult threshold to five symptoms, both of which
  widened adult recognition. Placed in this chapter rather than with the disruptive disorders —
  a deliberate statement that it is developmental, not behavioural.
- **Specific learning disorder** — one diagnosis with impairment specifiers in reading, written
  expression, or mathematics (each with sub-specifiers).
- **Motor disorders** — developmental coordination disorder, stereotypic movement disorder
  (specify with/without self-injurious behaviour), and the **tic disorders**: Tourette's
  (multiple motor + ≥1 vocal), persistent (chronic) motor *or* vocal, provisional (<1 year).

## Differentials worth getting right

- **Autism vs social (pragmatic) communication disorder** — the discriminator is restricted and
  repetitive behaviour. Present → autism. Absent, with pragmatic deficits → SPCD.
- **ADHD vs autism** — both affect attention and social functioning; they are frequently both
  present, and DSM-5 permits saying so.
- **Intellectual developmental disorder vs specific learning disorder** — global vs circumscribed.
- **Tics vs stereotypies vs OCD compulsions** — tics are faster, non-rhythmic, often preceded by
  a premonitory urge; stereotypies are rhythmic and typically earlier-onset; compulsions are
  goal-directed anxiety reduction ([[dsm-ocd-related]]).
- **ADHD inattention vs anxiety, depression, sleep deprivation, or trauma** — all produce
  attentional complaints, which is why onset and course matter more than the symptom list.

## Rails

Not a diagnostic instrument. **Never assign a diagnosis to the user or a third party** — explain
what a category means instead. Criteria require clinically significant distress or impairment
plus exclusion of substance/medication and other medical causes. If self-harm, suicidality,
abuse or acute crisis surfaces, drop the taxonomy and respond to the person: **988** (US/Canada
call or text), or local emergency services. Criteria text is copyrighted and not reproduced —
named only. Full rails in [[dsm-5]].

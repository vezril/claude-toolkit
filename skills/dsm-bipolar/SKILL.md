# Bipolar and Related Disorders

Placed **between** the psychotic and depressive chapters, which is the manual making a claim:
bipolar disorders bridge the two in symptomatology, family history and genetics.

Built from **episodes**, not from a continuous state. Get the episode definitions right and the
disorders follow.

## The episodes

| Episode | Duration | Core |
|---|---|---|
| **Manic** | ≥1 week (or any duration if hospitalised) | Elevated/expansive/irritable mood **and** increased activity or energy, most of the day, nearly every day |
| **Hypomanic** | ≥4 consecutive days | Same symptom picture, **observable by others**, but no marked impairment and no psychosis |
| **Major depressive** | ≥2 weeks | Depressed mood or loss of interest, plus associated symptoms |

**DSM-5 added "increased activity or energy" to Criterion A** for mania and hypomania. Mood
change alone no longer suffices — a change in activity is required. This was aimed at
over-diagnosis and it matters.

**Psychosis or hospitalisation makes it mania, not hypomania**, regardless of duration.

## The disorders

- **Bipolar I** — requires **one lifetime manic episode**. That's it. Depression is common but
  not required, and this catches people out constantly.
- **Bipolar II** — ≥1 hypomanic episode **and** ≥1 major depressive episode, never a manic one.
  Not a milder illness: the depressive burden is often greater and the manual says so
  explicitly.
- **Cyclothymic disorder** — ≥2 years (1 in children/adolescents) of hypomanic and depressive
  symptoms that never meet full episode criteria, present at least half the time, with no
  symptom-free stretch longer than two months.
- **Substance/medication-induced** — mania emerging during intoxication or withdrawal, or from
  a medication (steroids, antidepressants, stimulants, dopaminergics). The hard question is
  whether an antidepressant-triggered switch indicates underlying bipolarity; the manual lets
  persistence beyond the physiological effect support a primary diagnosis.
- **Due to another medical condition** — hyperthyroidism, Cushing's, MS, stroke, TBI.
- **Unspecified mood disorder** — **restored in DSM-5-TR** for presentations not yet separable
  into bipolar or depressive. Useful in exactly the acute situation where it was missing.

## Specifiers (they stack)

With anxious distress (rate severity) · **with mixed features** · rapid cycling (≥4 episodes in
12 months) · with melancholic features · with atypical features · with psychotic features
(mood-congruent or -incongruent) · with catatonia · with peripartum onset · with seasonal
pattern. Plus course: in partial/full remission, and current severity.

**The mixed-features specifier replaced DSM-IV's mixed episode.** Instead of requiring full
criteria for both poles simultaneously, sub-threshold opposite-pole symptoms are now flagged on
either a manic or a depressive episode — and it applies to [[dsm-depressive]] episodes too,
where it is a risk marker for bipolar conversion.

## Differentials

- **Bipolar II vs borderline personality disorder** — the mood instability of BPD shifts within
  hours and is usually interpersonally reactive; hypomania is sustained for days with changed
  sleep need and energy ([[dsm-personality]]).
- **Mania vs ADHD** — overlapping distractibility, talkativeness, activity; ADHD is chronic and
  developmental, mania is episodic ([[dsm-neurodevelopmental]]).
- **Mania vs stimulant intoxication** ([[dsm-substance-related]]).
- **Schizoaffective** — when psychosis persists outside mood episodes
  ([[dsm-schizophrenia-spectrum]]).
- **Irritability in children** — consider disruptive mood dysregulation disorder, which was
  added specifically to stop paediatric bipolar over-diagnosis ([[dsm-depressive]]).

## Rails

Not a diagnostic instrument. **Never assign a diagnosis to the user or a third party** — explain
what a category means instead. Criteria require clinically significant distress or impairment
plus exclusion of substance/medication and other medical causes. If self-harm, suicidality,
abuse or acute crisis surfaces, drop the taxonomy and respond to the person: **988** (US/Canada
call or text), or local emergency services. Criteria text is copyrighted and not reproduced —
named only. Full rails in [[dsm-5]].

# Depressive Disorders

Common feature: **sad, empty or irritable mood with somatic and cognitive changes** that impair
functioning. Separated from [[dsm-bipolar]] in DSM-5 — DSM-IV's single "mood disorders" chapter
was split, which is why the two chapters share episode definitions and specifiers.

## The disorders

- **Major depressive disorder** — ≥1 major depressive episode: ≥2 weeks, ≥5 of 9 symptoms,
  at least one being depressed mood or loss of interest/pleasure. Single or recurrent episode.
- **Persistent depressive disorder** — ≥2 years of depressed mood (1 year in children). **DSM-5
  merged dysthymia and chronic major depression** into this one category, because the
  distinction wasn't carrying its weight. DSM-5-TR continues to let full criteria for a major
  depressive episode be met during it.
- **Disruptive mood dysregulation disorder** — severe recurrent temper outbursts plus persistent
  irritable mood between them, onset before 10, diagnosed between 6 and 18. **Added expressly to
  reduce paediatric bipolar over-diagnosis** by giving chronic childhood irritability its own
  home. Cannot coexist with bipolar disorder or oppositional defiant disorder.
- **Premenstrual dysphoric disorder** — moved from an appendix into the main manual in DSM-5.
  Symptoms in the final premenstrual week, remitting after onset of menses, **confirmed by
  prospective daily ratings over ≥2 cycles** — the prospective requirement is the part usually
  skipped.
- **Substance/medication-induced** and **due to another medical condition** — the two rule-outs
  that are themselves diagnoses.

## The bereavement exclusion, removed

DSM-IV barred a major depressive diagnosis within two months of a death. **DSM-5 removed it**,
reasoning that grief and depression can coexist, that excluding it withheld treatment, and that
loss is not categorically different from other severe stressors. The manual adds extensive
guidance on distinguishing grief from a depressive episode: in grief, dysphoria comes in waves
tied to the loss and self-esteem is typically preserved; in depression, mood is persistent and
pervasive with worthlessness and self-loathing.

This remains the chapter's most contested change — flag it as contested rather than settled.
**Prolonged grief disorder** now lives in [[dsm-trauma-stressor]], added in DSM-5-TR.

## Specifiers

Same stack as [[dsm-bipolar]]: with anxious distress · **with mixed features** · with
melancholic features · with atypical features · with psychotic features · with catatonia · with
peripartum onset · with seasonal pattern · in partial/full remission · severity.

- **With mixed features** on a depressive episode is a **bipolar risk marker** — worth noticing,
  because it changes treatment thinking.
- **With peripartum onset** covers pregnancy *and* the four weeks postpartum. "Postpartum" alone
  understates it.
- **With anxious distress** was added in DSM-5 because anxiety predicts worse outcome and
  suicide risk.

## Differentials

- **Bipolar depression vs unipolar** — the question is always whether there has ever been a
  hypomanic or manic episode. Ask directly; patients rarely volunteer hypomania.
- **Grief** — see above; also uncomplicated bereavement in [[dsm-other-conditions]].
- **Medical:** hypothyroidism, anaemia, sleep apnea, vitamin D and B12 deficiency, Parkinson's,
  stroke, cancer, chronic pain. Fatigue and poor concentration are not specific.
- **Substance:** alcohol, sedatives, withdrawal from stimulants, interferon, isotretinoin,
  some hormonal contraceptives ([[dsm-substance-related]]).
- **Adjustment disorder** when a stressor is identifiable and criteria aren't met
  ([[dsm-trauma-stressor]]).
- **ADHD and depression** both present with poor concentration; onset and course separate them.

## Rails

Not a diagnostic instrument. **Never assign a diagnosis to the user or a third party** — explain
what a category means instead. Criteria require clinically significant distress or impairment
plus exclusion of substance/medication and other medical causes. If self-harm, suicidality,
abuse or acute crisis surfaces, drop the taxonomy and respond to the person: **988** (US/Canada
call or text), or local emergency services. Criteria text is copyrighted and not reproduced —
named only. Full rails in [[dsm-5]].

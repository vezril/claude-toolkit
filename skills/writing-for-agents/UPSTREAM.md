## Changes from upstream

Three additions to `SKILL.md`, each one measured on its own (v3, v4, v5 in the suite); `SKILL-MECHANICS.md` is upstream byte for byte.

- **Context pointers**: the "front-load the leading word" and "one trigger per branch" bullets each gained a worked example (descriptions kept synonym-padding and opening with "Use when…").
- **Steps and completion criteria**: the criterion is written into each step as its last sentence, including conditional steps and the final step (Sonnet skipped those two kinds of step).
- **Pruning**: a new bullet, "Keep the reason with its rule" (Haiku shortened rules' reasons away).

Built eval-first: the suite, the iteration log and every measured run are in `evals/writing-for-agents/`.

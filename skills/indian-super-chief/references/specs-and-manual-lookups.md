## Your configuration

**2022 Indian Super Chief** (base, *not* Limited) — the value/heritage cruiser of the reborn 2022 Chief line, with the touring lean (windshield, floorboards, passenger provisions) that separates "Super Chief" from the Chief/Chief Bobber.

- **Engine:** **Thunderstroke 111** — **1,811 cc (111 cu in)**, air-cooled (with oil cooling) **49° V-twin**, pushrod OHV, 2 valves/cylinder, closed-loop **electronic fuel injection**. Peak torque is published around **~120 lb-ft (~162 N·m)** at low rpm — treat that figure as approximate; confirm against Indian's spec sheet.
- **Valvetrain:** **hydraulic lifters** — i.e. **no scheduled valve-lash adjustment** on the Thunderstroke (self-adjusting). *(High-confidence platform fact; still worth confirming in your maintenance schedule.)*
- **Transmission:** **6-speed** constant-mesh, **wet multi-plate clutch**, **belt final drive** (Gates-type carbon-reinforced belt).
- **Cluster (yours = standard):** analog speedometer with a **digital inset** — **not** the 4-inch **Ride Command** TFT touchscreen (that's the Limited/Dark Horse tell). The inset shows gear position, odo/dual trip, fuel, clock, and telltales. This changes how you read trip/diagnostic info vs. Ride Command bikes.
- **Rider tech:** **keyless ignition** with an immobilizer/security system; **3 ride modes** (Tour / Standard / Sport — they remap throttle response, not power); **cruise control** (standard on Super Chief); **ABS** (your version).
- **Chassis:** steel-tube frame; telescopic fork front, single shock rear; single front disc + single rear disc with ABS; cast wheels (19" front / 16" rear on the base Chief line). Belt final drive is exposed on the left.
- **Fuel:** tank is **~4.0 US gal (~15 L)** — good but not huge; confirm exact capacity and reserve behavior in the manual.
- **Oil grade:** Indian specifies a **15W-60 full-synthetic** (their branded oil) for the Thunderstroke. *(Grade is well-established; capacity and change interval are must-verify — below.)*

## ⚠️ Must-verify numbers — do NOT guess these; here's where each lives

I'm deliberately not printing values for these. Look them up once and they're yours forever.

| What | Where to find the exact value |
|---|---|
| **Engine oil capacity** (and refill vs. dry-fill) | Owner's manual → *Specifications* / *Maintenance*. The Thunderstroke has a **sight glass** — check level per the manual's procedure (bike upright, warm/cold as specified). |
| **Transmission / primary oil** — separate fills exist on the Thunderstroke | Manual → *Specifications*. Do not assume it shares engine oil. |
| **Oil-change interval** | Manual → *Maintenance schedule*. Indian's pattern is a **break-in first service** (early miles) then **periodic changes** — confirm the exact mileage/time, don't assume "5,000." |
| **Tire sizes** | Read the **sidewalls** (front and rear differ) — authoritative for what's on the bike. |
| **Tire pressures (cold)** | The **VIN/tire placard** (frame or swingarm sticker) and the manual. **Set cold; never guess** — pressure is safety-critical. |
| **Belt tension / deflection spec** | Manual → *Maintenance*. Measure deflection at the specified point/load; over-tensioning damages bearings, under-tensioning risks skip. |
| **Spark plug type & gap** | Manual → *Specifications*. |
| **Fastener torques** (axle, caliper, drain plug, oil filter, pinch bolts) | **Service manual** torque tables. Safety-critical fasteners are exactly where "close enough" causes failures. |
| **Battery type/spec & fuse ratings/locations** | Manual → *Electrical*. Needed before you replace either. |
| **Coolant?** | There isn't one — it's **air/oil-cooled**, no radiator/coolant. (Stated so you don't go looking.) |
| **Fault/diagnostic codes** | The standard cluster can indicate faults, but **code meanings are dealer/service-manual territory** — see the troubleshooting reference; don't infer a code's meaning from its number. |

# 2022 Indian Super Chief — owner's companion

A troubleshooting and reference companion for **your specific bike**: a **2022 Indian Super Chief** (base trim) — **Thunderstroke 111** (1,811 cc air-cooled 49° V-twin), **6-speed + belt** drive, the **standard analog cluster** (not Ride Command), with **ABS**. It helps you understand the machine, decode a warning light, narrow a symptom, and decide *fix-it-yourself vs. dealer* — grounded in authoritative recall data and honest about everything it can't source.

> ## Two rules before anything else
>
> **1. Check your VIN for two open recalls — both apply to the Super Chief.** These are the fetched, authoritative findings, and both are crash-risk with **free** dealer fixes:
> - **I-22-06 (22V837000)** — **fuel pump may fail while riding** → engine stall. *The important one.*
> - **I-23-01 (22V957000)** — **kickstand may not retract** → catch in a turn.
> - Verify status by VIN at **nhtsa.gov/recalls** (US) or Transport Canada; or call Indian **1-877-204-3697**.
>
> **2. This is a companion, not the manual — and safety systems defer to a dealer.** I could not machine-retrieve Indian's manual (their site blocks it), so specs here are *orientation*, precision numbers are deliberately omitted (see below), and **brakes/ABS, fuel, steering, and tires are dealer/technician work.** Some symptoms mean **stop riding now** — those are flagged in the troubleshooting reference.

A motorcycle skill that invents a torque value or a tire pressure is worse than no skill — it gets someone hurt. So this one is built in tiers, and it tells you which tier any answer is on:

## Fast triage

| You're seeing… | First move |
|---|---|
| **Stalling / sputtering / hard hot-start** | **Check the fuel-pump recall (I-22-06)** by VIN — then dealer |
| **Any brake change** (longer stops, spongy, noise) | **Stop riding** — brakes are pass/fail; inspect before riding |
| **ABS light stays on** | Sensor if braking is normal; **stop-ride if braking feels off** |
| **Won't crank / dead dash** | Battery, kill switch, **kickstand up**, fob battery, fuse — owner-checkable |
| **Won't start but cranks** | Fuel + fob/immobilizer; recall check → dealer for EFI |
| **Check-engine light** | Don't ride hard; get the **stored code read** (meaning is dealer territory) |
| **Dies after sitting** | 12V battery + **battery tender** (parasitic drain is common here) |
| **Can't fill the tank** | The **filler-neck anti-siphon bar** — fuel slowly, shallow angle. Not a fault |
| **Kickstand behavior** | Confirm **recall I-23-01** is closed on your VIN |

## Owner-checkable vs. dealer (the boundary)

- **You, safely:** kill switch & interlocks (kickstand/clutch/neutral), keyless **fob battery** + backup start, **12V battery**/tender, **fuses**, **tire pressure** (cold, to placard), **oil level** (sight glass per manual), air filter, loose connections.
- **Dealer/tech, always:** brake & **ABS** hydraulics/sensors, **fuel system** (live recall area), **EFI/ABS fault codes**, charging-system diagnosis, safety-critical fastener torque, and **all recall work** (it's free — don't improvise it).

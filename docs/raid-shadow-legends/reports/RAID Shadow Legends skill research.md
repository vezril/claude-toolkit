# Build a RAID Coach That Labels Its Numbers

A Claude skill that advises on RAID: Shadow Legends needs five bodies of knowledge. The first is the combat model: a multiplicative damage chain, an accuracy margin of about 30 points over the target's resistance, affinity Weak Hits that block debuffs outright, and "true" speed with hidden decimals. The second is the gear system: nine slots, three variable main-stat slots, roughly sixty artifact sets, and the newer 1–9-piece variable sets that now dominate. The third is a playbook for every mode, built on the fact that each boss has one "solve" mechanic and a handful of stat thresholds. The fourth is a progression ladder. The fifth is a parser for the player's roster. The hardest design problem is not coverage. It is honesty. Plarium publishes almost none of the game's numbers, and its support pages refused automated fetches during this research. Everything the community "knows" therefore comes from fan sites of uneven age. Some numbers can be cited to current sources: the export schema (read from the extractors' C# source), the accuracy-versus-resistance curve, chest thresholds, boss stat tables and DeadwoodJedi's speed bands. Others are unverified folklore: 5★ and 6★ main-stat values, substat roll ranges, the turn-meter tick constant, the DEF mitigation formula, Classic Arena point thresholds and mastery scroll costs per tier. For the roster, the current HellHades extractor uploads to HellHades' servers and documents no local file. The skill should therefore target the open `artifacts.json` format (RaidExtractor / Raid Toolkit, `fileVersion` "1.3"). That file reports base stats only. It omits blessings, Awakening and artifact ascension, and it will show sets released after January 2024 under names no public table knows. The "what to work on next" engine should be the skill's own stated rubric: content value × role-gap fill ÷ remaining investment. HellHades' own scoring is not published. Throughout, the skill must parse only files the user hands it, never ask for game logins, never automate the game, and redact the account id and name.

## Three evidence labels separate facts from folklore

The source landscape decides what the skill may assert. The research ran on 2026-11-07, and the latest patch found was V11.90, which HellHades wrote up on 2026-11-03 ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/raid-update-11-90-unempowerment-wishlist-boost-ai-changes)). **Plarium's official support pages returned HTTP 403 to every automated fetch**. The RAID fandom wiki is thin and mostly last edited in 2022 ([Fandom wiki, General Artifacts](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/General_Artifacts)). That leaves three fan or creator sites as the working authorities, and they differ in kind rather than conflicting. **Ayumilove**, the user's primary source, is a broad catalogue that is updated often and aggregates community input; many of its details come from reader comments ([Ayumilove artifact guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/)). **HellHades** offers curated tier lists, an optimiser and mechanics explainers. **DeadwoodJedi** publishes exact true-speed Clan Boss tunes and a calculator ([DeadwoodJedi Basic tune](https://deadwoodjedi.com/speed-tunes/basic/)). Even HellHades' damage formula article is community reverse-engineering, not a Plarium statement ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/how-your-damage-is-actually-calculated-in-raid-shadow-legends/)).

This report and the skill should use three labels, and the skill should print the label next to every number it gives.

| Label | Meaning | How the skill phrases it | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[VERIFIED]` | Read directly from source code, or stated by a current (2025–2026) reference and not contradicted by another source. **It still does not mean official.** | States the number with its source and date | `artifacts.json` schema and enums; the typeId rule; ACC-vs-RES land rates; Clan Boss chest thresholds; Chimera stat table; Iron Twins stat bands |
| `[CHECK IN-GAME]` | Has a source, but only one fan source, a stale one (2022 or earlier), a small sample, or sources that conflict | "Community-reported (source, date). Confirm on the in-game screen." | Great Hall table; glyph ranges; upgrade odds; mercy thresholds; how Clan Boss affinity changes; Empowerment totals |
| `[UNVERIFIED]` | No source was found that states it | Declines to give a number and points to the in-game screen or a calculator | 5★/6★ main-stat values at +16; substat roll ranges; the turn-meter "SPD × 0.07" tick; the DEF constant; Classic Arena thresholds; mastery scroll cost per tier |

## Combat runs on a multiplicative chain, a 30-point accuracy margin and true speed

**Damage is a product, not a sum.** HellHades' chain multiplies these factors in turn: the scaling stat (ATK, or HP or DEF for champions that scale on those), the skill multiplier, the book bonus, a crit multiplier of (1 + C.DMG), mastery bonuses, affinity, buffs, passives, and finally DEF mitigation. `[VERIFIED as a community model, 2021]` ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/how-your-damage-is-actually-calculated-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). The practical consequence is that the weakest factor gives the most per point. That is why 100% crit plus high C.DMG plus Decrease DEF and Weaken beats stacking ATK alone. Decrease DEF works through mitigation and Weaken scales final damage, so the two multiply each other rather than overlapping. Damage-reduction passives also stack multiplicatively ([HellHades: Ally Protection](https://hellhades.com/ally-protection-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). **No DEF mitigation formula is verified.** The only one in circulation is a community fit, damage taken ≈ 1 − 0.85 × (1 − e^(−2·DEF/3000)). It surfaced through a search snippet attributed to HellHades comments and was not visible in the fetched article ([HellHades comments](https://hellhades.com/how-your-damage-is-actually-calculated-in-raid-shadow-legends/comment-page-2/)). The skill may describe DEF qualitatively, with diminishing returns, but it must not compute damage after DEF. `[UNVERIFIED]`

**Debuff landing follows a curve the skill can quote.** At equal ACC and RES the attacker lands about 90% of debuffs. With ACC 30 above RES it lands about 97%. With RES 100 above ACC the defender resists about 90%. And "unless an ability specifically states that it 'cannot be resisted', there is always a 3% chance for a resist" `[VERIFIED]` ([HellHades: Buffs, Debuffs and Instant Effects](https://hellhades.com/?p=2223)). The skill's own listed chance is checked before ACC versus RES. Content-specific rules repeat the same margin. On Chimera your ACC must exceed its RES by 25 to land debuffs, and your RES must exceed its ACC by 125 to resist its debuffs ([Ayumilove Chimera](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/)). On Spider, target about RES + 25, capped at 225 ([Ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)). A player rule of thumb of +25 for a 96–97% land rate agrees ([Plarium forum](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/674_game-discussion/1605789_accuracy-vs-resistance)). The default the skill should carry is **target RES + 25 to 30**.

**Affinity is the hidden reason many builds fail.** Magic beats Spirit, Spirit beats Force, and Force beats Magic. Void is neutral against everything. HellHades (2026-10-29) gives the advantaged attacker +15% C.RATE and a 30% chance of a Strong Hit (+30% damage, on non-crits only). The disadvantaged attacker deals −20% damage and has a 35% chance of a Weak Hit, a further −30% that also "prevents debuffs ... from being placed, regardless of how much ACC". Debuffs from skills with no damage component skip the Weak Hit check ([HellHades: How affinity works](https://hellhades.com/how-affinity-works-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). These are the newest figures found, but they differ from older community descriptions, and one secondary guide adds a crit penalty that HellHades does not list. Label them `[CHECK IN-GAME]` against the in-game Affinity help screen. The diagnostic order for a debuffer that "keeps missing" is affinity first, then ACC versus the stage's RES, then the skill's base chance.

**Speed fills the turn meter in proportion to SPD.** The champion furthest past 100% acts first. Set bonuses create hidden decimal speed, so two champions showing the same SPD can act in different orders ([HellHades: How speed works](https://hellhades.com/how-speed-works-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). That is why DeadwoodJedi lists **true speed** and tells players to convert displayed speed with its calculator ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/speed-tunes/basic/)). Ayumilove reports no speed cap and Arena speeds around 350 ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/?p=41741)). The widely repeated "turn meter fills SPD × 0.07 per tick" appears in no fetched source `[UNVERIFIED]`. The skill should never simulate turn order itself. It should hand tunes to the DeadwoodJedi calculator, which also warns against turn-meter and cooldown masteries such as Cycle of Magic and Lasting Gifts in fixed tunes ([DeadwoodJedi calculator guide](https://deadwoodjedi.com/clan-boss-calculator-guide/)).

Most buffs and debuffs come in two tiers, and Ayumilove's values can be carried as `[VERIFIED]` ([Ayumilove Buffs & Debuffs](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-buffs-debuffs-guide/)):

| Effect | Value | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decrease DEF | −30% / −60% | Increase ATK | +25% / +50% |
| Weaken | +15% / +25% damage taken | Increase DEF | +30% / +60% |
| Decrease ATK | −25% / −50% | Increase SPD | +15% / +30% |
| Decrease SPD | −15% / −30% | Increase C.RATE / C.DMG | +15% / +30% |
| Poison | 2.5% / 5% max HP per tick | Strengthen | −15% / −25% damage taken |
| HP Burn | 3% max HP, one per champion | Ally Protection | 25% / 50% redirected |

The 2024–2026 patches added counters that invalidate older guides. **Total Guard** stacks block one instance of damage each, and bosses ignore them; since V11.75 (2026-09-02), Provoke bypasses Total Guard. **Intercept** blocks crowd control. **Immutable** blocks cooldown increases, fully so since V10.70.2. **Keen Sight** arrived in V11.85 (2026-10-22) ([Ayumilove 2026 patch notes](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)). Bosses became immune to Berserk in V10.10 and to Ensnare, Fatigue and Hunter's Gaze in V10.90 ([Ayumilove 2025 patch notes](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2025/)).

Four masteries do most of the work in PvE. **Warmaster** has a 60% chance to deal 10% of the target's max HP, or 4% against bosses, once per skill. **Giant Slayer** has a 30% chance per hit to deal 7.5%, or 3% against bosses, so it suits multi-hit skills. **Lore of Steel** multiplies basic set bonuses by 1.15. **Eagle-Eye** gives +50 ACC ([Ayumilove Mastery Guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-mastery-guide/); [HellHades Masteries](https://hellhades.com/masteries-what-are-they-and-why-are-they-so-important/)).

## Champion power stacks in layers, and only some layers are cheap

A champion's power comes in layers that the recommendation engine must cost separately. **Rank** caps level at 10/20/30/40/50/60 for ranks 1 to 6. Ranking up takes N same-rank fodder (1 to reach R2, up to 5 to reach R6) and resets the champion to level 1. Legendaries start at R5 and Mythicals at R6 `[VERIFIED]` ([HellHades New Player Guide](https://hellhades.com/raid/new-player-guide/)). **Ascension** uses affinity potions from the Potion Keeps, and since V11.40 those keeps have permanent Super Raids ([Ayumilove 2026](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)). **Masteries** come from three trees, of which a champion can activate two, for a total of 15 masteries. A full set takes 100 Basic, 600 Advanced and 950 Divine scrolls from Minotaur. The first reset is free and later resets cost 150 gems `[VERIFIED]` ([Ayumilove Mastery Guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-mastery-guide/)). **Scroll cost per individual mastery tier was not found** `[UNVERIFIED]`. **Awakening** (Souls, from account level 42) and **Blessings** (five Divinities, with six levels tied to Awakening) arrived in Patch 6.0 ([Ayumilove Blessings](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-blessings-guide/)). **Empowerment** spends duplicates over four levels. Ayumilove's totals, for example +40% HP/ATK/DEF and +15 SPD on Legendaries, carry its own "verify in-game" caveat `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([Ayumilove Empowerment](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-empowerment-guide/)). New in V11.90, a Legendary can be Unempowered once a month with a single coin that does not carry over ([HellHades 11.90](https://hellhades.com/raid-update-11-90-unempowerment-wishlist-boost-ai-changes)). Account-wide layers sit on top of all this: the Great Hall, renamed "Affinity Bonuses" ([HellHades Area Bonuses](https://hellhades.com/how-to-use-area-bonuses-in-raid-shadow-legends/)), and Faction Guardians ([alucare](https://www.alucare.fr/en/ring-de-gardien-raid-shadow-legends/)).

The cheap-to-expensive order this implies for one champion is: 6★ and level 60, then masteries, then books on the skills that carry debuff chance or cooldown, then gear tuned to thresholds, then Awakening and Blessing, then Empowerment. HellHades' early-game rule supports the first step: "Get Your Starter to Level 60 ... Don't bother ranking up other champions until this is done" ([HellHades 10 Early Game Tips](https://hellhades.com/10-early-game-progression-tips-in-raid/)).

Resource bottlenecks shift with stage. Early on the limits are energy, XP and Silver. Mid-game they are books, scrolls, speed and accuracy gear, and Legendaries. Late game they are 6★ rolls, Souls and duplicates ([HellHades New Player Guide](https://hellhades.com/raid/new-player-guide/)). **Mercy (pity) numbers are contested.** Ayumilove's September 2026 table gives these thresholds, each followed by the increase per pull after it `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([Ayumilove Mercy](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-mercy-system-explained/)):

| Shard and rarity | Threshold | Increase per pull after |
|---|---|---|
| Ancient Legendary | 200 | +5% |
| Void Legendary | 200 | +5% |
| Sacred Legendary | 12 | +2% |
| Primal Legendary | 75 | +1% |
| Primal Mythical | 200 | +10% |

BlueStacks and others report different increments and counts ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/raid-shadow-legends/rsl-pity-system-guide-en.html)), and Plarium has published none. The skill can say that counters are tracked per shard type and that saving shards for boosted events pays. V11.75 raised regular boosts to 15x ([Ayumilove 2026](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)). It should never promise a guaranteed pull count.

## Gear decisions live on three variable slots and about sixty sets

Weapon, Helmet and Shield carry fixed flat main stats (ATK, HP and DEF). The build-defining choices sit on **Gloves** (C.RATE, C.DMG or a % stat), **Chest** (ACC, RES or a % stat) and **Boots** (SPD or a % stat), plus the Amulet (C.DMG or flat) and the Banner (ACC, RES or flat). SPD can be a main stat only on boots `[VERIFIED]` ([Ayumilove artifact guide, 2026-09-13](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/); [Fandom wiki](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/General_Artifacts)). **Rarity** sets the number of starting substats: Common 0, Uncommon 1, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4, and Mythical 4 with one already upgraded. **Rank** (stars) sets how large every stat is. Levels run to +16, and a substat is added or upgraded at +4, +8, +12 and +16 ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/); [GamerEmpire](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifacts-guide/)). **The only verified main-stat data point is DEF% boots: 4% → 30% at 3★, and 6% → 40% at 4★** ([GamerEmpire](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifacts-guide/)). The full 5★ and 6★ table was found nowhere. Commonly quoted values such as "6★ SPD boots = 45" are `[UNVERIFIED]`, and so are per-roll substat ranges. The wiki's Mythical page still lists Mythical start values as "??" ([Fandom wiki, Mythical](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical)). One trap needs a warning: an Ayumilove page that seems to list 5★–6★ base values is about a different game, Awaken Chaos Era, and must not be used ([ayumilove.net/?p=70426](https://ayumilove.net/?p=70426)).

**Gear Ascension** (December 2022) adds a bonus stat layer. Per a quoted snippet of a Plarium page, it needs Rank 4+ and level 12+ gear and costs Oils and Extracts rather than Silver ([HellHades Ascension guide](https://hellhades.com/artifact-and-accessory-ascension-guide/); [Plarium snippet](https://plariumplay-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/8020641151516-Basic-Guide-Gear-Overview)). **Glyphs** replace rather than stack, and every value in a glyph's range is equally likely ([Plarium Glyphs, via snippet](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014708619-Glyphs)). The wiki's glyph ranges date from 2022, the page is marked a stub, and its figures contradict each other `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([Fandom](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Enchanting_Artifacts)). Crowd-sampled upgrade odds fall from 77% at the 4→5 step to 5% at 15→16. The last figure rests on 5 successes in 92 tries `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([Fandom Upgrade Chance](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrade_Chance)). HellHades' levelling rule is operational and safe to repeat: take Rare gear to +8, continue to +12 only if it rolled well, and treat one useful SPD roll as useful while three or four make a piece "account-defining" ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/?p=25622); [HellHades: How speed works](https://hellhades.com/how-speed-works-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). Recent quality-of-life changes alter the advice. Removing gear became free in V10.60.5. Auto-Equip arrived in V11.50 (2026-05-06). Storage rose to 2,200 and Anomalous Accessories were added in V11.70 ([Ayumilove 2025](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2025/); [Ayumilove 2026](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)).

Variable sets count both artifacts and accessories, from 1 to 9 pieces. A recommender should therefore express a build as "N pieces of X plus a 2-piece Y" rather than assuming the old 4+2 layout. Ayumilove's per-piece values for most variable sets were not captured, and its Stoneskin listing contradicts itself. Even its tier list holds an evident error: Mercurial's anchor points at Merciless ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/)).

From the slot rules and stat priorities, three archetype templates follow. This table is a synthesis rather than a quoted prescription.

| Archetype | Gloves | Chest | Boots | Amulet / Banner | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATK nuker | C.RATE (C.DMG once crit is capped) | ATK% | SPD | C.DMG / ATK | 100% C.RATE, then C.DMG and ATK |
| HP or DEF scaler | C.RATE | HP% / DEF% | SPD | C.DMG / HP or DEF | Same, on the scaling stat |
| Debuffer | HP% / DEF% | ACC (or % stat if ACC is met) | SPD | flat / ACC | ACC to threshold, then SPD |

**Reading an Ayumilove champion page is a parsing task.** The Arbiter page (updated 2026-10-18) follows a fixed template. It opens with a header (faction, rarity, role, affinity), then star ratings per mode (Arena offense and defense, each clan boss, Faction Wars, each dungeon and its Hard version, Doom Tower floors and bosses), skills with multipliers, and then a build block. The build block gives PvE, PvP and "PvE & PvP" set lists, the main stat per variable slot, and a stat priority grouped by role. Separate PvP and PvE mastery tables come last ([Ayumilove Arbiter](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-arbiter-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)). A set row is **a menu of acceptable sets, not one loadout**. Ayumilove tells the skill *which* stats and sets to use. It rarely says *how much*, so thresholds must come from mode guides, HellHades' stage tools and DeadwoodJedi.

## Each boss has one solve mechanic and a few hard thresholds

### Clan Boss rewards unkillable speed tunes and a 70.27M top chest

The Demon Lord has six difficulties, Easy through Ultra-Nightmare (UNM), and holds at most 10 debuffs. Keys respawn every 6 hours, for up to four free hits a day ([HellHades Ultimate CB Guide](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/); [Ayumilove CB](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)). Gathering Fury ramps the boss's damage from turn 10. From turn 50 it ignores Block Damage and Unkillable and applies Block Revive to the champions it kills ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)). It is immune to Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Decrease SPD and Turn Meter reduction, among others. UNM boss speed is 190 ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/building-your-first-ultra-nightmare-clan-boss-team/)). HellHades says to prioritize DEF over HP "99 of 100 times", and to spend Great Hall medals on ACC first, then DEF ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)).

**How the boss's affinity changes is a live conflict** `[CHECK IN-GAME]`. The wiki (2025-10-26) and HellHades (2026-10-29) describe each day starting as Void and switching affinity once the boss falls below 50% HP ([Fandom Clan Boss](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Clan_Boss); [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/how-affinity-works-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). DeadwoodJedi's tunes, by contrast, give per-day advice such as "sub Ninja out on Force days" ([DeadwoodJedi Myth Eater](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2632)). No source gives a fixed calendar. The skill should ask the user for today's affinity rather than predict it.

Chest thresholds are among the best-corroborated numbers in the game. Ayumilove and DeadwoodJedi agree on the top-chest cut-offs `[VERIFIED]` ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/); [DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=3472)). The minimum champion stats come from one source and are guidance `[CHECK IN-GAME]`. InTeleria puts UNM debuffers at 230+ ACC ([InTeleria](https://www.inteleria.com/raid-speed-tuned-clan-boss-teams/)).

| Difficulty | Top chest (one clan-day) | Ayumilove minimum SPD / ACC / DEF / HP |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | >1.142M | 90 / 20 / 1,000 / 22,500 |
| Normal | >3.638M | 120 / 50 / 2,100 / 27,500 |
| Hard | >11.648M | 140 / 70 / 2,400 / 30,000 |
| Brutal | >21.694M | 160 / 140 / 2,700 / 32,500 |
| Nightmare | >39.166M | 170 / 170 / 3,000 / 35,000 |

Team-building divides at whether the player owns an unkillable or block-damage source. **Traditional teams** need Decrease ATK above all, plus poison or HP Burn, speed above the boss, counterattack, and healing or Lifesteal. HellHades' free-to-play starter team is Kael, Apothecary, Marked, Coffin Smasher and Frozen Banshee ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)). **Unkillable teams** are the standard route to UNM. DeadwoodJedi's Ultimate Budget Unkillable uses one set of true speeds for UNM and NM on all affinities and syncs on turn 3 `[VERIFIED as published; confirm in the DWJ calculator]` ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2591)):

| Slot | True speed | Slot | True speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maneater | 256–257 | DPS 2 | 179–185 |
| Pain Keeper | 222–226 | Stun Target | 129 |
| DPS 1 | 187–189 | | |

The tune bans Relentless. It also forbids Counterattack accessories and masteries on Pain Keeper, and turn-meter or cooldown sets and masteries on the two core champions. It wants Warmaster on every DPS and one Brimstone, one Cruelty and otherwise Phantom Touch blessings. The Stun Target should have the highest HP on the team, but no more than 125% of Pain Keeper's, and DEF several hundred below everyone else ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2591)). Myth Eater (Maneater 286, Demytha 171, Ninja at 224–230 in a 4:3 slot, DPS at 179 and 160–169) syncs on turn 6 on UNM ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2632)). Budget HeartKeeper needs only Trunkheart and Pain Keeper ([DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=5549)). DeadwoodJedi notes that its listed speeds sometimes differ from its own calculator, so **the skill should route every tune through the calculator** rather than treat the table as gospel. Selecting a tune is then a lookup on the core pair the roster contains: Maneater + Pain Keeper, Maneater + Demytha, or Trunkheart + Pain Keeper. With none of these, the fallback is a traditional poison team.

### Hydra is a head-counter puzzle before it is a damage race

Hydra gives 3 keys a week and fields 6 champions against four of six heads, with two more waiting as substitutes ([Ayumilove Hydra](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)). Its caps decide which damage sources work `[VERIFIED]`. Max-HP damage is capped at 10% of a head's max HP. HP Burn is capped at 20,000 per tick. Reflect, Warmaster and Giant Slayer are each capped at 50,000 per attack. A decapitated head becomes an exposed neck, and a regrown head carries 75% damage reduction. The skill should first ask which heads are up this rotation and then fill the counter slots before any damage slot ([HellHades Titus guide, 2025-09-12](https://hellhades.com/titus-blackplume-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)):

| Head | Threat | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Torment | Fear, then hits every Feared enemy; 90% poison reduction | Perfect Veil or regular debuff removal |
| Decay | Heal Reduction; heals cut your max HP | Provoke |
| Blight | Stacked poisons and Leech | Block Buffs |
| Suffering | AoE Weaken; +50 RES to all heads; Provokes your top-C.DMG champion | Block Buffs |
| Mischief | Steals buffs and turn meter; redirects attacks | A high-RES self-buffer "tank"; Hex |
| Wrath | AoE Decrease ATK; Vengeance at 15 hits | Block Buffs |

The leading 2025 damage plan scales Titus Blackplume's A2 off Shield size. It pairs him with Wixwell as the shield grower (Bloodshield accessories are the budget substitute) and three buff extenders such as Krisk, Mad Hatter, Demytha or Lady Mikage. Trunda remains the top single damage dealer ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/titus-blackplume-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)). **Shield-scaling teams have been nerfed twice before**, around Corpulent Cadaver and Yannica. The skill should present Titus as "current as of September 2025, check for changes". Top chests require >6.66M (Normal), >20.4M (Hard), >29.4M (Brutal) and >36.6M (Nightmare) in weekly damage ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)). HellHades' "at least 215 ACC on Normal" target (head RES 140) is about 2.5 years old, and HellHades itself points readers to its Stages Tool `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/getting-started-with-hydra-clan-boss-in-raid-shadow-legends/)).

### Chimera scores Trials, not just damage

Chimera is the third clan boss, which HellHades called "currently final" in November 2025, and the only source of the Mythical champion Embrys ([HellHades Chimera](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/chimera/)). Players field 5 champions for 65 turns with 2 keys a week. The boss cycles U → R → U → L → U → V → U every 5 turns, where U is the Ultimate Form and R, L and V are the Ram, Lion and Viper forms, and it wipes its buffs and debuffs on each return to Ultimate. Ram, Lion and Viper share one affinity per 28-day rotation ([Ayumilove Chimera](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/)). Ayumilove publishes a full stat table `[VERIFIED]`. At UNM the boss has 1.61B HP and 305 SPD, the Ultimate Form has 400 RES, and Ram reaches 450 RES and 500 ACC. Combined with the RES + 25 rule, that gives a concrete ACC target per form. The UNM top chest is >112.5M ([Ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/)). Because completed form Trials earn rewards and Clash points but also empower the Ultimate Form, a Chimera team is a toolkit. Ram wants Fear, Weaken and Decrease DEF, plus Block Debuffs or Intercept against its stuns. Lion wants Shields, Increase DEF, Block Damage, and counterattack or Ally Attack. Viper wants Block Active Skills, a reviver and Decrease ACC. Named Chimera teams could not be read: the HellHades sections are rendered from its optimiser and came back empty.

## PvP rewards speed in Classic Arena, drafting in Live Arena and breadth in Siege

**Classic Arena** is asynchronous. Each player sets one defense, and the weekly reset falls on Monday at 08:00 UTC ([Fandom Arena](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Arena)). Platinum is the top 500 worldwide. Since 10.80.0 its cutoff has been Sunday 21:00 UTC ([HellHades 10.80.0](https://hellhades.com/raid-shadow-legends-update-10-80-0/)), and it is the only source of the Swift Parry and Deflection gear. Wins pay Great Hall medals and Magisteel ([HellHades Beginner's Guide, 2026-03-20](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). HellHades' 2026 archetypes are speed (a booster, an SPD aura lead, control and a nuker; the best choice for new players), blender, go-2nd (Stoneskin, Chronophage, Feral or Mercurial), cut-in (Supersonic) and stall/revive. Speed matters less in Platinum ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)). Arbiter's +30% SPD aura is Arena-only and does not apply in Siege. **Point thresholds per tier, weekly reward tables and arena tier stat bonuses were not found** `[UNVERIFIED]`, and the Gold V threshold appears as both 3,200 and 3,500 in HellHades snippets `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/?p=26240)). **Tag Team Arena** requires three defenses and two wins out of three, unlocks at level 35, and pays Gold Bars for tomes ([Ayumilove Raid Guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-guide/)).

**Live Arena** is a real-time draft: alternating picks, one ban each, then leaders, with unique picks required from Gold upward. Seasons unlock at Gold I (2,700 points) and run four weeks on and two off. A win is worth +9 season points and a loss −3. The top milestone, 100 Mythical relic fragments, needs 1,320 points, and 10 battles a day are free `[VERIFIED, Mar 2025]` ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/how-to-get-all-the-live-arena-season-rewards/)). The early-2026 meta is defined by **Mercurial**. A 9-piece set gives 3 Total Guard stacks at round start, and each stack blocks damage, debuffs, crowd control and buff removal. Multi-hit skills, counterattacks, Reflect, ally attacks and Increase Skill Cooldown counter it ([HellHades, 2026-03-12](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-is-changing-live-arena-fights/)). V11.75 then let Provoke bypass Total Guard ([Ayumilove 2026](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)). Bayek on Mercurial with Fabian, Galleus and Karnage was a highlighted core ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/busted-arena-combo-try-this/)). PvP advice dated before 2025 is history.

**Siege** (Patch 9.0, July 2024) runs every two weeks: a 12-day prep phase, then a two-day battle on Tuesday and Wednesday. It has 18 Posts with 3 random Conditions each, and the requirements are 15 members at level 45, 50 activity stars and 5 defense teams ([HellHades Patch 9.0](https://hellhades.com/patch-9-0-update-clan-siege-is-live/); [Plarium forum](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/673_guides-and-tutorials/1714524_siege)). Rewards are Lamasu fragments, Feral and clan relics. **Clan vs Clan** runs about every second week, and the standard play is to save books, ascensions, 6★ rankings, Great Hall medals and Tag Team tokens for it. HellHades' point values date from 2021, so the skill should point users to the in-game Objectives tab ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/how-to-farm-clan-vs-clan-points-updated/)).

The **Great Hall** spends Classic Arena medals on permanent per-affinity HP%, ATK%, DEF%, C.DMG, RES and ACC. Upgrades are irreversible. Medals convert at 1 Gold = 2 Silver and 1 Silver = 2 Bronze ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)). The wiki's 10-level table tops out at 20% HP/ATK/DEF, 25% C.DMG and 80 RES/ACC, but it dates from 2022 and its costs look low `[CHECK IN-GAME]` ([Fandom Great Hall](https://raid-shadow-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Hall)). Priority advice conflicts at the margin, but every source starts with ACC ([GamerEmpire](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-great-hall-guide/)).

## The roster file is the open artifacts.json, not the HellHades upload

**The current HellHades extractor is a closed pipe into HellHades' servers.** Its troubleshooting guide (2024-12-17) describes only "Upload to Website", which "effectively replaces your account info in the optimiser", and documents no save-to-file option ([HellHades Troubleshooting Guide](https://hellhades.com/hellhades-optimizer-troubleshooting-guide/)). Remote Extraction runs on HellHades' servers using the player's Raid login and 2FA code ([HellHades Optimiser 3.0](https://hellhades.com/introducting-raid-optimiser-3-0-now-with-mobile-and-remote-support/)). Since August 2024 the extractor also **writes to the game** through Auto-Equip and Auto-Sell ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/optimiser-update-biggest-changes-ever/)). The documented local format is `artifacts.json`, from LukeCroteau's RaidExtractor (Gen 1, archived, last pushed 2021) or from the Raid Toolkit extractor (Gen 2), whose model sets `fileVersion = "1.3"` ([RaidExtractor](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor); [raid-toolkit/extractor](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/extractor); [RTK AccountDumpClient.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/DataModel/GameData/AccountDumpClient.cs)). No raid-toolkit repository has been pushed since February 2024 ([GitHub API](https://api.github.com/orgs/raid-toolkit/repos)), and whether RTK still works on the 2026 client is unknown.

The schema was read from the C# source, so it carries the `[VERIFIED]` label. The file is one UTF-8 JSON object serialized by Newtonsoft with camelCase keys. It is written as `artifacts.json` or as a `.zip` containing that entry. Gen 2 exports **only the first account** ([Gen 2 Program.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/extractor/blob/main/Program.cs)).

| Level | Keys | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Top | `fileVersion` (Gen 2 only), `id`, `name`, `lastUpdated`, `artifacts[]`, `heroes[]`, `arenaLeague`, `greatHall`, `factionGuardians` (Gen 2), `shards`, `stagePresets` | `id` and `name` identify the account and are personal data |
| Artifact | `id`, `kind` (slot), `rank` ("One"…"Six"), `rarity`, `setKind`, `level` (0–16), `primaryBonus`, `secondaryBonuses[]`, `requiredFraction` (optional), `sellPrice`, `failedUpgrades` | **No owner field** |
| Bonus | `kind`, `isAbsolute` (true = flat), `value`, `enhancement` (glyph), `level` (rolls into this substat) | |
| Hero | `id`, `typeId`, `name`, `grade` ("Stars1"…"Stars6"), `level`, `empowerLevel` (Gen 2), `awakenLevel`, `fraction`, `rarity`, `role`, `element`, `artifacts[]` (equipped ids), `masteries[]`, `skills[]{id,typeId,level}`, `inStorage`, `isGuardian`, base stats | Mastery-scroll dictionaries appear in Gen 2 only |

Sources: [Gen 1 AccountDumpClient.cs](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor/blob/master/RaidExtractor.Core/AccountDumpClient.cs); [Gen 2 Extractor.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/Account/API/Extractor.cs).

Five encoding rules matter more than the rest. **First, percentages are fractions.** The extractors divide the game's 32.32 fixed-point value by `uint.MaxValue` and round to 2 decimals, so 15% arrives as `0.15`, and a 4.6% roll collapses to `0.05` ([Gen 1 Extractor.cs](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor/blob/master/RaidExtractor.Core/Extractor.cs); [ModelExtensions.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/Account/ModelExtensions.cs)). **Second, the typeId rule.** Across all 4,748 entries of the bundled `hero_types.json`, `id == baseId + awakeLevel` and `baseId % 10 == 0`. Gen 2 computes `AwakenLevel = TypeId % 10`. So `typeId // 10 * 10` identifies the champion and `typeId % 10` gives the **ascension** stars ([hero_types.json](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor/blob/master/RaidExtractor.Core/hero_types.json)). The field named `awakenLevel` is ascension, not Awakening. **Third, hero stats are base stats only.** They are scaled for rank and level but exclude gear, masteries, the Great Hall and the ascension form. One research note described them as "final stats", but the source code is unambiguous and wins ([Gen 1 Extractor.cs](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor/blob/master/RaidExtractor.Core/Extractor.cs)). **Fourth, a lot is missing.** The RTK model holds blessings, Awakening rank (double ascension), artifact ascension and resources, but **none are copied into the dump** ([RTK Hero.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/DataModel/GameData/Hero.cs); [RTK Artifact.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/DataModel/GameData/Artifact.cs)). Relics do not appear in the documented schema either. **Fifth, set names drift.** The newest public enum (2024-01-31) lists 62 internal set names but lacks Mercurial, Chronophage, Supersonic, Feral, Zeal, Rebirth, Merciless and Slayer. It even disagrees with the C# model on rarity, which C# spells `Mythical` and TypeScript `Mythic` ([Artifacts.ts](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/Client/Node/src/Types/Artifacts.ts); [Enums.ts](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/Client/Node/src/Types/Enums.ts); [ArtifactRarityId.cs](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/account-extension/blob/main/DataModel/Enums/ArtifactRarityId.cs)).

No file pairs internal set names with their in-game names. The best available mapping is a high-confidence inference: `AttackSpeed` = Speed, `Hp` = Life, `AttackPower` = Offense, `LifeDrain` = Lifesteal, `DecreaseMaxHp` = Destroy, `GetExtraTurn` = Relentless, `CooldownReductionChance` = Reflex, `HpAndHeal` = Immortal, `ShieldAnd*` = the Divine sets. Two research notes disagree on `IgnoreDefense`: one says Savage, the other Cruel. Mapping it to Savage and `AttackPowerAndIgnoreDefense` to Cruel matches the set bonuses (Savage ignores 25% DEF; Cruel gives +15% ATK and ignores 5% DEF), but it is still `[UNVERIFIED]` until a real file confirms it.

## Implications for the skill's design

### Target artifacts.json 1.3 and treat HellHades as a non-source

The skill should accept `artifacts.json` in both generations, detect a `.zip` and open its `artifacts.json` entry, and offer a typed or CSV roster as the fallback. It should say plainly that the current HellHades extractor documents no exportable file. If a user supplies some other HellHades payload, the skill should treat it as unknown rather than force it into the `artifacts.json` shape. Version detection is simple: no `fileVersion` means Gen 1, and `"1.3"` means Gen 2, which also carries `empowerLevel`, `isGuardian`, `id` and `name`.

### A tolerant parser survives drift

| Hazard | Required tolerance |
|---|---|
| Unknown `setKind` strings (post-2024 sets) | Show the raw string verbatim. Map only through a dated, labelled table. Never reject the file. |
| Unknown `fraction`, `rarity` or stat `kind` values (newer factions such as Shadowkin and Sylvan Watchers; `Mythical` vs `Mythic`; an `IgnoreDefense` substat) | Normalize the rarity spellings to one value. Pass other unknowns through. |
| Missing keys (`NullValueHandling.Ignore` drops nulls; `requiredFraction`, `artifacts` on unequipped heroes, the Gen 2-only fields) | Treat every key except `id` as optional. |
| Fractions vs percents | Multiply by 100 when `isAbsolute` is false. If a "%" value exceeds 1, assume it is already a percent and flag it. Warn that values are rounded to 2 decimals. |
| typeId | Base id = `typeId // 10 * 10`; ascension = `typeId % 10`. Join to outside data by normalized `name`, because public typeId tables are 2 to 6 years stale ([raidchamps/static-data](https://github.com/raidchamps/static-data)). |
| Ownership | Build a reverse index from `heroes[].artifacts`. Anything unlisted is inventory. Recover the slot from the artifact's own `kind`. |
| Effective substat | Use `value + enhancement` (the glyph). This is inferred, so check it on the first real file. |
| Multi-account | Gen 2 holds only the first account. Say so. |

### Read the file for what it has; look up only what it lacks

A key insight cuts the unverified surface sharply: **the skill does not need the unverified main-stat or substat tables**, because the file holds the actual rolled value of every piece. "Is this a good piece?" can be judged by roll count (`bonus.level`) and by which stats the rolls landed in, the HellHades levelling logic, rather than by roll size against unknown ranges.

| Computed from the file | Looked up (dated and cited at runtime) | Asked of the user |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership, rank, level, ascension, empowerment | Champion kits, roles and per-mode ratings (Ayumilove stars, HellHades 0–5), by name | Blessings, Awakening rank, relics |
| Mastery count and which T6 masteries (by id) | The Ayumilove build: sets, main stats, stat priority, masteries | Artifact ascension bonuses |
| Skill levels (max levels must be looked up) | Set bonuses and the set-to-farm map | True in-game total stats for key champions |
| Gear per champion, set counts, SPD-roll inventory, sell value | Skill max levels; Great Hall per-level values (2022 table) | Content cleared; clan's CB, Hydra and Chimera difficulty |
| Per-faction roster depth (Faction Wars) | Mode mechanics and thresholds from this report | Today's CB affinity; current Hydra heads; Doom Tower rotation |
| Great Hall level sums; guardians; shard counts | DeadwoodJedi tunes (and the calculator itself) | Goals and where they are stuck |
| Approximate total stats (base + gear + sets + Great Hall + known masteries), always labelled approximate | Patch changes, nerfs and reworks | |

What the skill must never compute: turn order (defer to the DeadwoodJedi calculator), damage after DEF (no formula), and Clan Boss affinity (ask the user).

### Safety and ToS rails

Both open exporters read game memory. Gen 1 calls `OpenProcess` read-only and `ReadProcessMemory` ([Gen 1 Extractor.cs](https://github.com/LukeCroteau/RaidExtractor/blob/master/RaidExtractor.Core/Extractor.cs)). RTK is unsigned and asks users to "trust the developer(s)" ([RTK Security Notice](https://github.com/raid-toolkit/raid-toolkit.github.io/blob/HEAD/pages/installation/security.md)). The only claim that such tools are allowed is a 2022 HellHades paraphrase of Plarium ([HellHades](https://hellhades.com/raid-shadow-legends-guide-tips-cheats-and-strategies/)). Plarium's rules for its other titles forbid play "by means that go beyond the usual game process" ([Plarium Vikings rules](https://vikingsweb-support.plarium.com/js/en/pages/in-game-rules)), and a Plarium forum thread questions whether remote extraction complies ([Plarium forum](https://forum.plarium.com/en/raid-shadow-legends/674_game-discussion/1446860_hh-raid-optimizer---remote-extraction/)). The rails follow directly. The skill **parses only a file the user provides** and never tells them to install or run a memory reader; if they ask, the tool choice and its ToS risk are theirs. It **never asks for or accepts Plarium logins or 2FA codes**, which rules out recommending Remote Extraction; HellHades' own disclaimer warns against sharing login details ([HellHades disclaimer](https://hellhades.com/disclaimer/)). It **never automates the game**, never scripts equipping or selling, and never steers users to third-party auto-equip or auto-sell. It **redacts the top-level `id` and `name`** from every output, log and quoted snippet, and it never uploads the file anywhere. It also states the file's blind spots (base stats only; no blessings, Awakening or artifact ascension) so its recommendations never overstate what it can see.

### A "what to work on next" heuristic the skill can defend

HellHades publishes the inputs but not a formula. Its tier list rates champions 0 to 5 per area and admits the scores are "not set in stone" ([HellHades Tier List](https://hellhades.com/raid/tier-list/)). ## Conclusion

The skill's real job is to join three sources that never meet on their own. The roster file says what the player owns. Ayumilove says which sets, stats and masteries suit each champion. DeadwoodJedi, HellHades and the mode guides say how much of each stat a piece of content demands. The in-game screen stays the final authority over all three. That framing also contains the unverified-number problem better than any lookup table could. Because the file already carries every piece's actual rolled values, the riskiest folklore, the 6★ main-stat and substat-range tables, is not needed at all. What remains unverified (the turn-meter tick, DEF mitigation and arena thresholds) belongs to calculators and in-game screens, and the skill should hand those questions off rather than answer them.

The larger risk is drift, not error. Sets introduced after January 2024 will arrive as strings no public table maps. Shield-scaling Hydra teams have already been nerfed twice. Total Guard changed within months of Mercurial's release. RTK itself has been silent since early 2024. A skill that dates every lookup, tolerates unknown values, and asks the user for the facts the file cannot carry will stay useful as the game changes. A skill that bundles a static ratings snapshot will start giving confident, wrong advice within a few patches.

# RAID: Shadow Legends PvP and clan-competitive modes (Classic Arena, Tag Team Arena, Live Arena, Siege, CvC, Great Hall)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Every claim carries its source's publication date in brackets where known. The PvP meta moves fastest, so anything dated before 2025 should be treated as historical. fandom.com wiki pages returned HTTP 402 and could not be read. ayumilove's arena tier pages were linked but their contents were not retrieved.

## Classic Arena: tiers, rewards, meta archetypes, counters, speed, budget teams

### Takeaway
Classic Arena is asynchronous and played weekly. You set one defense and attack other players' defenses. Weekly rewards depend on your tier, and wins pay Great Hall medals and Magisteel. Platinum is the top 500 players worldwide and is the only source of the Swift Parry and Deflection accessories. The archetypes in 2026 are speed/nuke, blender, go-2nd (Stoneskin/Chronophage/Feral/Mercurial), cut-in, and stall/revive. Speed matters most below Platinum.

### Cited Findings
**Structure and tiers**
- The brackets are Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Tiers I–IV apply to every bracket except Platinum [Jan 2021] — [HellHades: Understanding the Arena](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)
- Gold V and Platinum exist only in Classic Arena, not Tag Team Arena — [HellHades (search snippet, undated)](https://hellhades.com/?p=26240)
- The Gold V threshold conflicts between sources. One HellHades post says it was lowered from 3,500 to 3,200 points. Another says Gold V runs from 3,500 to 3,800 points. Neither change could be dated — [HellHades search snippets](https://hellhades.com/?p=25030); [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/?p=26240)
- Each player sets one defense and attacks other players' defenses through the week. Rewards are paid by tier at week's end. The wins and tier rewards are Great Hall Medals (for Affinity Bonuses) and Magisteel (Forge crafting) [Mar 20, 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide to Arena and Siege](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Platinum is reserved for the top 500 players in the world. It is the only source of the Swift Parry and Deflection accessories [Mar 20, 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Update 10.80.0 [Oct 7, 2025] moved the Platinum cutoff to Sunday 21:00 UTC, 12 hours before the Monday 08:00 UTC weekly reset. After the cutoff you can still fight for Medals and Magisteel, but you cannot gain points, change your Platinum rank, or promote from Gold V to Platinum. Plarium said the old timing favored certain time zones — [HellHades: Update 10.80.0](https://hellhades.com/raid-shadow-legends-update-10-80-0/)
- Medals per win scale by tier. Examples: Silver I pays 1 Silver Medal, Silver IV pays 3 Silver Medals, Platinum pays 5 Gold Medals. Conversion is 1 Gold = 2 Silver = 4 Bronze — [Gamer Empire / mobi.gg search snippets (undated)](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-great-hall-guide/); conversion confirmed by [HellHades (2021)](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)
- Weekly reward amounts rise with the rank you hold at the end of the period — [mobi.gg](https://mobi.gg/en/tips/arena-raid-shadow-legends)

**Meta archetypes (HellHades, Mar 20, 2026)** — [Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- **Speed team:** a turn-meter booster, an SPD aura lead, a control or buff-removal champion, and a nuker. HellHades calls it the top performer for new players.
- **Blender:** a turn-meter booster, an ally-attack champion, and two AoE-A1 champions such as Skullcrown and Sinesha. The A1 champions can run zero speed.
- **Go-2nd:** survives the opponent's opening turns using Stoneskin, Chronophage, Feral or Mercurial gear, plus passives such as Niamhe's or Spear of Nyresa's.
- **Cut-in:** fast control champions on Supersonic gear, often paired with Stoneskin, built to act before the enemy speed booster resolves.
- **Stall:** tanky heal and revive teams that are very hard to kill. Used in both Arena and Siege.
- HellHades says speed matters less in Platinum Arena, where go-2nd and stall defenses are common.
- Duchess Lilitu is still described as an arena-defense "queen", especially alongside Komidus or Cheshire Cat — [Inteleria: best champions for arena defense (undated)](https://www.inteleria.com/best-champions-for-arena-defense/)
- Mythical champions still largely dominate Classic Arena standings [Mar 2025] — [PCGamesN tier list](https://www.pcgamesn.com/raid-shadow-legends/tier-list)

**Counters and mechanics**
- Debuffs (Weaken, Decrease DEF, Block Buffs) and control (Stun, True Fear, Freeze, increased cooldowns) break teams. Go-2nd beats speed teams, cut-in beats turn-meter boosters, and focus fire removes key threats [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Classic speed-lead structure: a lead with an "All Battles" or "Arena" SPD aura, then two boosters, then two damage dealers. The go-second alternative uses a DEF or RES aura with heavy defensive builds [2021] — [HellHades: Understanding the Arena](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)
- Arbiter's aura gives +30% SPD in Arena battles only. Arena-only auras do not apply in Siege — [HellHades Beginner's Guide (2026)](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Team Power never guarantees a win. Turn order and synergy matter more — [Gamer Empire Arena Guide (undated)](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-arena-guide/)

**Speed per bracket**
- The only figure found is an anecdote: a player "stayed in Gold IV at around 320 SPD" on the lead, in an old Plarium forum thread. It is very likely outdated — [Plarium forum (search snippet)](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/674_game-discussion/219213_best-arena----remake-classic-arena)

**Budget / F2P teams**
- ayumilove's F2P arena list [page updated Oct 31, 2026]: High Khatun (SPD aura and Increase SPD, moves first), Spirithost (SPD aura and Increase ATK, moves first), Warmaiden (Decrease DEF, moves second), Athel or Kael (starter nukers, third or fourth), Berserker (attacker, third or fourth), Relickeeper (finisher). ayumilove also advises skipping fights you expect to lose — [ayumilove Raid Guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-guide/)
- ayumilove keeps separate "Arena Defense" and "Arena Offense" champion rankings. Their contents were not retrieved — [ayumilove Raid Guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-guide/)

### Inferences
- For a new player the practical goal is a speed team (SPD lead, booster, debuffer, nuker). It farms Silver and Gold medals daily, and the medals feed the Great Hall, which helps PvE too.
- Platinum (top 500) is an endgame whale space. For most accounts, a reliable Gold IV/V finish is the realistic weekly target.
- Revive teams built on Arbiter, Duchess Lilitu, Cardiel, Siphi or Lyssandra fit HellHades' "stall" and "go-2nd" archetypes. Their counters follow from the general rules: Block Revive (not explicitly sourced here), heal reduction, Increase Cooldown, and burst through Stoneskin with multi-hit skills.

## Tag Team Arena (3v3)

### Takeaway
Tag Team Arena works like Classic, but you set three defenses and must win two of three fights. It needs a much deeper roster and has no Gold V or Platinum. It pays Gold Bars for the Tag Team Bazaar (skill tomes, champion fragments, forge materials), and its tokens are a classic CvC point source.

### Cited Findings
- You set three defenses at once. It needs many more champions than Classic, so it is less accessible, and there is no Platinum tier. Rewards are Bars for the Tag Team Bazaar [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- You must win 2 of 3 matches. Gold Bars buy Skill Tomes, Champion Fragments and Forge Materials — [ayumilove Raid Guide (updated Oct 2026)](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-guide/)
- It unlocks at level 35. Historically the bazaar offered a daily Drexthar Bloodtwin fragment for 100 days [2021, may be outdated] — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)
- In CvC, Tag Team Arena points scale with gold bars per win, so higher leagues are worth more. HellHades advises spending all Tag Team Arena tokens during CvC and running a weak defense so you keep winning easy games [2021] — [HellHades: How to farm CvC points](https://hellhades.com/how-to-farm-clan-vs-clan-points-updated/); [HellHades 2023 CvC guide snippet](https://hellhades.com/2023-clan-vs-clan-tournament-guide/)

### Inferences
- Tag Team Arena's main progression value is skill tomes from the bazaar. For mid-game accounts it is often the best repeatable tome source, though the current bazaar stock was not verified.

### Gaps
- Current bazaar inventory and prices, the current featured bazaar champion, and Tag Team tier thresholds were not found.

## Live Arena: format, seasons, rewards, speed, meta

### Takeaway
Live Arena is real-time and drafted. Players alternate picks, each bans one enemy champion, then each chooses a leader. Champions must be unique from Gold tier. Seasons run 4 weeks on and 2 weeks off once you reach Gold I (2,700 pts). A win is +9 and a loss is −3 season points, and the top milestone (1,320 pts) pays 100 Mythical relic fragments. The early-2026 meta is defined by the 9-piece Mercurial set (Total Guard). Bayek, Nais the Shadowthief and Fabian are prominent, and multi-hit, counterattack and Increase-Cooldown champions are the counters.

### Cited Findings
**Format**
- Draft: alternating picks, then each player bans one enemy champion, then each picks a team leader. From Gold tier every pick must be unique (no mirrors). It is open only in set daily time slots, and Plarium planned 24/7 access sometime in 2026 [Mar 20, 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Ban logic: ban the enemy's key champion. Banning Arbiter slows a speed team, and banning Fabian stops a cut-in team [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)

**Seasons and rewards** [Mar 29, 2025] — [HellHades: How to get all the Live Arena season rewards](https://hellhades.com/how-to-get-all-the-live-arena-season-rewards/)
- Seasons unlock automatically at Gold I (2,700 points). You cannot drop out of Gold or opt out once unlocked. Each season is 4 weeks followed by a 2-week off-season. Season points, milestones and leaderboard rewards fully reset each season.
- Season points: +9 for a win, −3 for a loss. The top milestone, 100 Mythical relic fragments, needs 1,320 season points. After that, a Seasonal Chest drops every 175 points and contains the Stonecleaver and Rebirth gear sets.
- You get 10 free battles a day. The first refill costs 10 gems, and later refills cost more. At a 50% win rate you need about 15 battles a day to reach the top milestone. At 60%, the 10 free battles plus some refills is enough.
- Gold-tier bots are AI copies of random accounts. They draft weaker teams, always ban your first pick, and are served after 3 straight losses, which limits point bleed.
- Your first 10 fights each season match on your non-seasonal rank, then on season points. Early-season matchmaking is chaotic, so HellHades suggests saving refills for the second half.
- Overall rewards: Great Hall Medals for **Area Bonuses** (separate from the Classic Arena affinity bonuses), Live Arena Relic Fragments, Starstone and Basalts, Primal Shards, and the Zeal, Impulse, Stonecleaver and Rebirth gear sets [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Live Arena wins earn points toward Great Hall Area Bonuses — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-guide/)

**Meta (early 2026)**
- How Mercurial works [Mar 12, 2026]: a 4-piece set gives 1 Total Guard stack at round start, 6 pieces give 2, and 9 pieces give 3. A champion starting its turn at 0 stacks gains 1. Total Guard blocks damage, debuffs, crowd control and buff removal, and only Increase Skill Cooldown gets through. Each damaging hit removes one stack, as do Ally Protection damage and Reflect Damage. Bosses ignore it. Faster champions recover stacks sooner. Mercurial extends fights and cuts one-turn kills, and it makes Polymorph a weaker Blessing — [HellHades: Mercurial is changing Live Arena fights](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-is-changing-live-arena-fights/)
- Mercurial counters: triple-hit and double-hit skills, counterattacks (which remove a stack even when the hit is blocked), Reflect Damage, ally-attack skills, and Increase Skill Cooldown. Weak into it: single-hit, slow, or redirect-heavy champions — [HellHades, Mar 2026](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-is-changing-live-arena-fights/)
- Mercurial adoption: start with fast revivers and debuff supports, then move to damage dealers who can spare a set bonus. A full 9-piece set often beats perfect substats. Raglin, Nell and Karnage appear in example builds — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-is-changing-live-arena-fights/); [HellHades Mercurial set page](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-artifact-set/)
- Bayek "thrives in the Mercurial meta" because he applies debuffs safely and is hard to remove. The suggested core pairs Bayek on Mercurial with Fabian (lockout), Galleus and Karnage (damage), and Hilvi or Noldua (fight extension and control) [Mar 3, 2026] — [HellHades: busted arena combo](https://hellhades.com/busted-arena-combo-try-this/)
- A top player's (Nub's) 20 Live Arena champions [Jan 11, 2026] credit Nais the Shadowthief as a core reason his win rate jumped. The full list was not retrieved — [HellHades: Nub Raids top 20 arena champions](https://hellhades.com/nub-raids-top-20-arena-champions/)
- A Vortex Gaming report mentions an upcoming Mercurial nerf and stresses banning or controlling enemy Provoke champions when running Mercurial. It is undated and unconfirmed by primary patch notes — [Vortex Gaming](https://vortexgaming.io/en/postdetail/1238014)
- An older 2023 drafting guide built around Harima, Rotos and Georgid is historical only — [HellHades (2023)](https://hellhades.com/?p=36643)

### Inferences
- Counter-pick logic: hold your answer to their likely win condition until you have seen their picks. Answer Mercurial with multi-hit, counterattack or Increase Cooldown champions. Ban the speed booster (Arbiter) against speed teams, or the cut-in enabler (Fabian). Unique picks from Gold upward make first-pick priority matter.
- Live Arena's Area Bonus medals and Mythical relic fragments make it a progression source even at a 50% win rate. The top milestone can be reached with daily refills.

## Siege: structure, conditions, rewards, team building

### Takeaway
Siege launched in July 2024 with patch 9.0 and runs every 2 weeks. It has a 12-day prep phase, under a day of matchmaking, and a 2-day battle on Tuesday and Wednesday. Clans set defenses in buildings (the Stronghold, Defense Towers holding 2–4 teams, Mana Shrines and others) and on 18 Posts with 3 random Conditions each. Winning needs more Victory Medals plus the enemy Stronghold. Rewards are Authoratrix Lamasu fragments, the Feral set, and Clan relic fragments. Siege rewards roster breadth, and Arena-only auras do not work there.

### Cited Findings
- Phases: Prep (12 days: set defenses, upgrade and repair buildings, set bonuses and conditions), then Matchmaking (under a day), then Battle (2 days), then Results Calculation. The first Siege started July 11, 2024 — [HellHades: Patch 9.0 Clan Siege is live (Jul 10, 2024)](https://hellhades.com/patch-9-0-update-clan-siege-is-live/)
- Siege runs every 2 weeks, with battles on Tuesday and Wednesday. The number of defenses depends on building levels. Your attack count depends on the enemy's building levels and your clan rank. Victory goes to the clan that takes down more defenses plus the enemy Stronghold [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Participation requirements: 15 clan members at level 45, 50 Clan Activity Stars in the last 7 days, and 5 Defense Teams set during prep [2024] — [HellHades Patch 9.0](https://hellhades.com/patch-9-0-update-clan-siege-is-live/)
- Buildings: the Stronghold sets the level of all other buildings, and capturing or defending it is a key win condition that pays extra rewards to all members — [The Ria Games](https://theriagames.com/?p=98780). Defense Towers hold 2–4 Defense Teams depending on level and can apply buffs to themselves only. Buffs cost Mana Orbs, and higher-level buffs cost more — [Gameleap](https://gameleap.com/articles/raid-shadow-legends-siege-clan-fortress-details)
- Posts: there are 18 per fortress, each with 3 random Conditions drawn from a fortress-wide pool, affecting every champion fighting there. Posts are the only place members can set Conditions — [Plarium forum Siege guide](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/673_guides-and-tutorials/1714524_siege)
- In practice [Jan 25, 2026]: Mana Shrines "swung the score dramatically" and Defense Towers "became win conditions". You get one chance per post. A Rank 6 vs Rank 26 war was decided by 100 points out of nearly 5,000. Stoneskin, Feral, Lethal, Bolster and 9-piece sets all matter, as do counterattacks, revives, Polymorph immunity and A1 extra turns. A clan used 47 different champions across 20 battles. Champions can be regeared mid-war — [HellHades: Why Raid's most intense game mode is also its best](https://hellhades.com/why-raids-most-intense-game-mode-is-also-its-best/)
- Team building versus Arena: Arena is dominated by "a handful of meta champions", while Siege rewards breadth, creativity and adaptability. "You can't plug in 350-speed Arbiter and call it a day" [Jan 2026] — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/why-raids-most-intense-game-mode-is-also-its-best/). Arena-only auras such as Arbiter's +30% SPD do not work in Siege, so use "All Battles" auras [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Rewards: Victory Chests with Authoratrix Lamasu (Legendary) fragments, scaled by Siege tier, plus Victory Medal milestones [2024] — [HellHades Patch 9.0](https://hellhades.com/patch-9-0-update-clan-siege-is-live/). Siege is also the best source of Clan Relic Fragments, and it pays the Feral gear set, Clan gold and Clan XP [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)

### Inferences
- Siege rewards a deep, flexible roster: many mid-tier champions geared for specific conditions, with All-Battles SPD auras. Investing in a second-string PvP roster pays off here in a way it does not in Classic Arena.
- Lamasu is the headline Siege-only champion. Fragment accrual depends on your clan's Siege tier, so clan quality affects how fast you get her.

- Whether the Siege shop holds champions beyond Lamasu (for example a rotating shop) was not found.

## Clan vs Clan tournaments and progression timing

### Takeaway
CvC runs roughly every second week. Clan members earn points through ordinary progression (books, ascensions, 6-star rankings, Great Hall upgrades, Forge crafting, Clan Boss chests, Spider, Faction Wars, Tag Team Arena), and some activities are doubled per tournament. The standard advice is to save books, ascensions, 6-star rankings, Great Hall medals and Tag Team tokens, and spend them during the tournament. Exact current point values live in the in-game Objectives tab.

### Cited Findings
- Tournaments run about every second week. Clan tier is set by your points percentile in the previous tournament. Which activities are doubled changes per tournament. Personal Rewards sometimes add bonuses for a win. The full point list is in the Objectives tab, and HellHades runs a CvC calculator — [HellHades 2023 CvC Tournament Guide](https://hellhades.com/2023-clan-vs-clan-tournament-guide/); [HellHades CvC Calculator](https://hellhades.com/raid/clan-vs-clan-calculator/)
- Point values [Oct 2021, likely changed since]: Legendary book 3,000, Epic book 1,000, Rare book 500. Ranking a champion to 6★ 1,000. Ascending a Legendary from 5 to 6 2,500. Spider 20/25 200–300 per run. Faction Wars stage 21 ×8 runs gives 8,000 per day with two factions. Great Hall upgrades are worth "lots" at level 10. Clan Boss chests pay passive points, Forge crafting pays points, and a Tier 6 final milestone was 5.4M (about 180k per member) — [HellHades: How to farm CvC points (updated)](https://hellhades.com/how-to-farm-clan-vs-clan-points-updated/)
- Timing tactics: save books, Great Hall medals (especially toward level-10 bonuses), late-stage ascensions, duplicates for 6★, and Forge crafts for CvC. Hit Clan Boss the day before and during the event. Burn all Tag Team Arena tokens during CvC with a weak defense — [HellHades 2021](https://hellhades.com/how-to-farm-clan-vs-clan-points-updated/); [HellHades 2023](https://hellhades.com/2023-clan-vs-clan-tournament-guide/)
- Points do not transfer if you switch clans mid-tournament — [CyberPost](https://cyberpost.co/how-often-are-clan-vs-clan-raid-shadow-legends/)
- CvC tournament improvements shipped Oct 6, 2023 — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/raid-shadow-legends-update-october-6-2023-cvc-tournament-improvements)

### Inferences
- For progression planning, CvC often overlaps fusion events and summon events. Batch ranking, fusion fodder, ascension and Great Hall spending into the CvC window, as long as it does not delay a time-limited fusion deadline.
- Great Hall medals spent in CvC count twice: once for CvC points and once for the permanent stat bonus.

### Gaps

## Great Hall, Arena gear rewards, and PvP-relevant artifact sets

### Takeaway
The Great Hall (unlocks at level 6) uses Classic Arena medals plus Silver to buy permanent stat bonuses per affinity (HP, ATK, DEF, C.DMG, RES, ACC). It is irreversible and expensive. The common priority is ACC first, then RES, DEF and HP for survival, with C.DMG ahead of ATK. Live Arena medals fund a separate set of Area Bonuses. PvP gear rewards include Swift Parry and Deflection accessories (Platinum only), Zeal, Impulse, Stonecleaver and Rebirth (Live Arena), and Feral (Siege). Mercurial, Stoneskin, Chronophage and Supersonic define the 2026 meta.

### Cited Findings
- The Great Hall unlocks at level 6, and upgrades cannot be reverted — [Plarium forum basic Great Hall guide](https://plarium.com/forum/en/raid-shadow-legends/673_guides-and-tutorials/594610_basic-guide-great-hall/)
- The Arena is the only way to improve the Great Hall [2021] — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-arena-and-the-benefits-it-can-bring/)
- Priority guidance conflicts. One guide recommends ACC, then C.DMG, RES, DEF, HP, ATK. A player on the Plarium forum finished ACC and then moved to RES. Another guide shifts priorities if you already run DEF sets — [Gamer Empire Great Hall guide](https://gamerempire.net/raid-shadow-legends-great-hall-guide/); [Plarium forum](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/674_game-discussion/205561_great-hall-2)
- A community calculation found that maxing one affinity takes an enormous number of medals. That is a forum estimate, not official — [Plarium forum: are arena medals worth grinding](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/674_game-discussion/203137_are-arena-medals-worth-grinding-for---if-you-have-71-years--then-maybe----beware-some-math-here-)
- Live Arena medals fund Great Hall **Area Bonuses**, which are separate from the Classic affinity bonuses — [HellHades Beginner's Guide (2026)](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/); [HellHades: how to use Area Bonuses (Apr 2023)](https://hellhades.com/how-to-use-area-bonuses-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- PvP reward gear: Swift Parry and Deflection accessories from Platinum only. Zeal, Impulse, Stonecleaver and Rebirth from Live Arena. Feral from Siege. Magisteel from Classic Arena for the Forge [Mar 2026] — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Sets that define the meta [2026]: Stoneskin, Chronophage, Feral and Mercurial (go-2nd), and Supersonic (cut-in) — [HellHades Beginner's Guide](https://hellhades.com/beginners-guide-to-arena-and-siege-the-pvp-modes-in-raid-shadow-legends/). Mercurial (9-piece variable set, Total Guard) is the biggest meta shift of early 2026 — [HellHades Mar 2026](https://hellhades.com/mercurial-is-changing-live-arena-fights/). Mercurial was introduced alongside 8 new relics in the Grim Forest content — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/new-mercurial-gear-set-and-8-new-relics-coming-with-grim-forest/)

### Inferences
- Recommended Great Hall order: ACC (for the affinities you debuff with most) and RES (to resist enemy control in PvP) give the most value early. DEF and HP help go-2nd and stall teams. Spend medals during CvC for double value.
- Arena medals turn into permanent account power, which makes daily Classic Arena one of the most important long-term "PvP rewards that matter for progression".

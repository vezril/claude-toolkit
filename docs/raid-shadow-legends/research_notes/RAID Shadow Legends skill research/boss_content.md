## 1. Clan Boss (Demon Lord): difficulties, affinity, mechanics, chest thresholds, team archetypes, speed tunes, substitutions

### Takeaway
Demon Lord has six difficulties (Easy through Ultra-Nightmare) and a 10-debuff cap. Gathering Fury ramps its damage from turn 10, and from turn 50 it ignores Unkillable and Block Damage. Speed-tuned "unkillable" teams, built on Maneater plus either Pain Keeper or Demytha, are the standard route to UNM. They run on true-speed bands from DeadwoodJedi: for example, Ultimate Budget Unkillable uses Maneater 256-257, Pain Keeper 222-226, DPS 187-189 and 179-185, and Stun Target 129. Top chest on UNM (Transcendent) needs more than 70.27M damage across a clan day.

### Cited Findings

**Access, keys, difficulties**
- The difficulties are Easy, Normal, Hard, Brutal, Nightmare and Ultra-Nightmare (UNM). UNM is where the top rewards come from: sacred shards and legendary books. — [HellHades Ultimate CB Guide](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- Each fight costs 1 Clan Boss Key. Keys respawn every 6 hours, for up to 4 free hits a day. Extra keys cost 100 Gems, and spare keys can go into one fight to add up total damage. — [ayumilove CB guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/); [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- Clan Boss is the main regular source of shards, books, and Cruel and Immortal gear. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)

**Skills (all affinities)**
- The A1, Crushing Force, hits one enemy with an unresistable 1-turn Stun. Its damage scales with the target's max HP. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)
- The other two skills depend on affinity:
  - Void: Flesh Wither (2.5% Poison for 2 turns) and Dark Nova (hits all).
  - Force: Belittle (25% Decrease ATK for 2 turns).
  - Magic: Swirling Darkness (25% Decrease ACC for 2 turns).
  - Spirit: Numbing Fear (15% Decrease SPD for 2 turns).
  - Force, Magic and Spirit all use Crash Through: hits all enemies 4 times and gives the boss 25% Increase ATK for 2 turns, on a 3-turn cooldown.
  — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/); [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- Infernal Resilience reduces damage from Poison, HP Burn and max-HP-based skills, with caps that vary by difficulty. HellHades' author says in the comments that poison caps at 50,000 per tick on the highest tiers, and that Poison Sensitivity raises the cap. Only one HP Burn is useful; poisons stack to the debuff limit. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- The boss holds a maximum of 10 debuffs, so extra poisons are wasted. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/); [DeadwoodJedi first UNM team](https://deadwoodjedi.com/building-your-first-ultra-nightmare-clan-boss-team/)
- Gathering Fury raises the boss's skill damage each turn from turn 10, faster from turn 20. From turn 50 its attacks ignore Block Damage and Unkillable, and champions it kills get Block Revive. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)
- Immunities: Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Block Active Skills, Fear, True Fear, Petrification, Decrease SPD, max HP reduction, Turn Meter reduction, HP exchange, HP balancing and cooldown increase. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)
- UNM boss speed is 190. DeadwoodJedi's classic turn order for a non-unkillable team: debuffer 195, buffer 194, healer 193, damage dealers 191-192. — [DeadwoodJedi](https://deadwoodjedi.com/building-your-first-ultra-nightmare-clan-boss-team/)
- Weaken makes every hit on the boss deal 25% more damage. — [ayumilove, via search snippet](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)

**Affinity (sources conflict)**
- HellHades' Ultimate guide says the boss doesn't buff itself while Void for the first 50% of its HP. Once it becomes Magic, Spirit or Force it gains a large attack increase. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- ayumilove advises bringing mixed-affinity backups for when the boss drops below 50% HP, and gives no daily schedule. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-clan-boss/)
- DeadwoodJedi's tunes are labelled "all affinities" and include affinity-specific advice: sub Ninja out on Force days, and choose the Stun Target by weak affinity. That only makes sense if affinity is a property of the day or fight. — [DWJ Myth Eater](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2632); [DWJ Ultimate Budget Unkillable](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2591)
- A search summary of older guides says the boss starts Void each day and switches to Force, Magic or Spirit below 50% clan HP until reset. I found no authoritative statement of the daily rotation order. — [HellHades Guide to CB (older)](https://hellhades.com/hellhades-guide-to-clan-boss/)

**Team roles for traditional (non-unkillable) teams**
- Primary roles:
  - Decrease ATK, the most important.
  - Poison or HP Burn.
  - Speed above the boss.
  - Counterattack (Valkyrie, Martyr, Skullcrusher).
  - Healing, or Lifesteal gear on the whole team.
- Secondary roles: Weaken early and Decrease DEF late; Ally Protection; Increase DEF; debuff extenders.
- Turn-meter helpers: Apothecary, High Khatun, Diabolist.

Source: [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/). DeadwoodJedi puts Ally Protection first for traditional teams and names poison as the main damage source, with Kael the champion to get for it. — [DWJ](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=3472)

- HellHades' free-to-play starter team: Kael (poisons), Apothecary (heal and turn meter), Marked (Decrease and Increase DEF), Coffin Smasher (Decrease ATK and HP Burn) and Frozen Banshee (poisons and Poison Sensitivity). Banshee build: Lifesteal set, DEF ring, DEF amulet with ACC substat, ACC banner, speed boots. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/ultimate-clan-boss-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/)
- DeadwoodJedi rates Poisons the single best damage source and pairs HP Burn with them. Geomancer's passive works off HP Burn. Turn meter boosts come from Apothecary or Deacon Armstrong. — [DWJ](https://deadwoodjedi.com/building-your-first-ultra-nightmare-clan-boss-team/)

**Unkillable archetypes and exact speeds (DeadwoodJedi; all speeds are true speeds without speed auras; verify in the calculator)**

*Ultimate Budget Unkillable* (Maneater + Pain Keeper + 2 DPS + Stun Target). The same speeds work on UNM and NM, all affinities, and sync on turn 3. Source: [DWJ](https://deadwoodjedi.com/?p=2591).

| Slot | Speed |
|---|---|
| Maneater | 256-257 |
| Pain Keeper | 222-226 |
| DPS 1 | 187-189 |
| DPS 2 | 179-185 |
| Stun Target | 129 |

Openers:
- UNM: Maneater opens with A3. Pain Keeper goes A1, A2, A1, A3.
- NM: Maneater goes A2, A3. Pain Keeper goes A1, A1, A3, A2.

Stun Target rules:
- Highest HP on the team, but no more than 125% of Pain Keeper's HP. DWJ suggests 5-10% above.
- DEF several hundred below everyone else's.
- Weak affinity against the boss, or nobody on the team should be weak. Avoid a strong-affinity Stun Target.
- Be careful with shield champions such as Kreela or Lonatharil in the Stun Target slot.
- Recommended setups: a Void Stun Target with four interchangeable DPS (two each of one affinity), or two Stun Targets matched to your DPS affinities.

Restrictions and setup:
- No Relentless.
- No Counterattack accessories or masteries on Pain Keeper.
- No turn-meter or skill-timing sets or masteries on Maneater or Pain Keeper.
- Blessings: 1 Brimstone, 1 Cruelty, the rest Phantom Touch.
- Warmaster on all DPS.
- An Ally Attack champion can fill a fast DPS slot: on UNM open with the Ally Attack skill, on NM open A1 first.

### Inferences
- The skill can map a roster to a tune by its core pair:
  - Maneater + Pain Keeper → Ultimate Budget Unkillable.
  - Maneater + Demytha → Myth Eater or the Myth Heir family.
  - Trunkheart + Pain Keeper → Budget HeartKeeper.
  - No Maneater → a traditional poison team. This is a "Seer + whatever"-style tune or 1:1 tune if the player has a block-damage or unkillable source, otherwise a Decrease ATK + Ally Protection + poison team.
- Speeds are exact true-speed bands, and auras, Lowest-of-Speed mastery and speed sets shift them. The skill should always tell users to confirm in the DeadwoodJedi calculator (deadwoodjedi.info/cb/…) rather than trust listed numbers. DWJ itself notes listed speeds sometimes differ from its calculator; one search snippet notes Maneater at 240 vs 256 on Ultimate Unkillable.
- For the 1-in-turn stun, the tune design and the Stun Target rules show that the A1 stun goes to a predictable champion based on HP and affinity. The rules above are the practical guide; I found no source text stating the exact targeting algorithm.

- No authoritative daily affinity rotation order. The 50%-HP switch claim conflicts with the per-day affinity framing in DWJ tunes. Verify in game or on the Plarium wiki.
- I could not read the Batman, Demytha-only, "Seer + whatever", No-CA and dedicated HP Burn tunes. The DWJ index pages 404'd, and HellHades and other sources had no speeds for them.
- No dated list of tunes invalidated by champion reworks in 2025-2026. DWJ tune pages carry no update dates.

## 2. Hydra: heads, mechanics, difficulties, scoring, archetypes, key champions

### Takeaway
Hydra is a weekly clan boss with 3 keys, teams of 6 and four difficulties (Normal to Nightmare). Four heads start the fight and two wait as substitutes, drawn from Torment, Decay, Blight, Suffering, Mischief and Wrath. A head at 0 HP is decapitated, becomes an exposed neck taking 200% more damage, and triggers an effect; a replacement grows back with 75% damage reduction. The Hydra can devour a champion, who is lost unless you deal enough damage to the digesting head in time. Shield-scaling damage around Titus Blackplume is the leading 2025 approach.

### Cited Findings
**Access and rules** (source: [ayumilove Hydra](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/))
- 3 Hydra Keys a week, weekly reset, 6 champions per battle. The sixth slot exists partly because the Hydra can devour one.
- Free Regroup lets you test teams: damage doesn't count and neither keys nor champions are spent.
- Champions used in a battle are locked until weekly reset unless you own duplicates.
- Chests are claimable only from your original clan, with a 6-hour lockout after switching clans.
- Head affinities stay fixed for the month. ayumilove is inconsistent on whether the head mix changes weekly or monthly.

**Shared passives (all heads)**
- Damage from enemy-Max-HP skills is capped at 10% of the head's Max HP.
- HP Burn is capped at 20,000 per tick.
- Reflect, Warmaster and Giant Slayer damage is capped at 50,000 per attack.
- Heads are immune to Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Block Skills, Fear, True Fear and Petrification.

— [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)

**Heads and decapitation effects** (source: [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-hydra-clan-boss-guide/))

| Head | What it does | When decapitated |
|---|---|---|
| Torment | A1 has a 50% chance of 2-turn Fear and then hits every Feared enemy. A2 (3-turn CD) is an AoE doing +25% per debuff on each target. Passive: True Fear on attackers, 90% poison reduction, 10% AoE reduction. | Removes Fear and gives Perfect Veil to 2 random allies. |
| Decay | A1 hits the lowest-HP enemy with 100% Heal Reduction. A2 (4-turn CD) clears all head debuffs and fills turn meters by 30%. Rotten Ward (5-turn CD) cleanses the lowest-HP head and grants a 4-turn Life Barrier. Passive: each heal you receive cuts your Max HP by 15% of the heal, up to 75%. | Heals your team 30% and restores 50% of the lost Max HP. |
| Blight | A1 is an AoE applying three 5% Poisons and a 75% Leech chance. A2 detonates poison on Leech-marked targets. A3 gives Poison Cloud to the heads. Passive: takes double HP Burn damage. | Damages heads under HP Burn. |
| Suffering | A1 applies unresistable AoE 25% Weaken. A2 gives 50% Ally Protection to the other heads, 30% Reflect to itself, and Provokes your highest-Crit-Damage champion. A3 applies Pain Link and takes an extra turn. Passive: +50 RES for all heads, 30% AoE reduction, 90% poison reduction. | Shields your team for 30% of Max HP. |
| Mischief | A1 hits the most-buffed enemy, stealing its buffs and 50% of its turn meter. A2 spreads its buffs to all heads. Passive: 75% chance to redirect attacks to another head, except while it is Hexed. | Empties all heads' turn meters. |
| Wrath | A1 is an AoE 50% Decrease ATK. A2 is an AoE Provoke and gives itself Increase ATK. Vengeance: at 15 hits received it gets 300% damage, Block Debuffs and 15% Reflect. | Damages all heads, more if it was under Vengeance. |

**Regrowth and devour**
- A regrown substitute head has Serpent's Will: 75% incoming-damage reduction.
- Mark of the Hydra can't be removed or blocked. The devouring head becomes Digesting, with extra HP and a timer. If you don't deal enough damage before the timer runs out, the devoured champion is lost permanently. ayumilove doesn't give the values.

**Counters per head (HellHades)**
- Mischief: steals buffs, so build RES on the carry or bring a high-RES self-buffer as a "Mischief tank".
- Decay: needs Provoke.
- Torment: needs Perfect Veil or regular debuff removal.
- Blight, Suffering and Wrath: need Block Buffs.
- Hex adds damage.

— [HellHades Titus Hydra guide, Sep 12 2025](https://hellhades.com/titus-blackplume-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)

**Titus shield team (HellHades)**
- Titus Blackplume is the second-best Hydra damage dealer, behind Trunda. His A2 scales with Shield size.
- Shield sources: Shield gear set, champion abilities, and the Volcardiac Heart relic. One source works; two roughly double the damage.
- Shield growers: Wixwell is the strongest. Brogni is weaker and needs a high-crit, high-damage build. Bloodshield accessories are the fallback, and they snowball after the first decapitation.
- Buff extenders: run three on a 3-turn cooldown so Titus's Shields never expire. Options are Krisk, Mad Hatter, Demytha and Lady Mikage. Lanakis can fill in with Reflex, Impulse or Merciless sets.
- Support roles: Decrease ATK, Strengthen and Increase DEF make Shields take less damage. Decrease DEF, Weaken and Reduce Skill Cooldown raise damage. 9-piece Protection on buffers and 9-piece Feral on debuffers add damage.
- Maximum damage needs Wixwell and Titus using A2 every turn through cooldown reduction. The top version uses Lamasu and Yumeko and must be played manually.
- Auto-friendly versions stack Lady of Ireth, Emic Trunkheart and Fjorad Wolfheart for smaller cooldown reductions.

— [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/titus-blackplume-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)

**Balance risk (invalidation flag)**
- Earlier Shield-scaling teams built around Corpulent Cadaver and Yannica were both nerfed. Plarium has said it won't nerf Titus; HellHades notes the community is skeptical and recommends a backup plan. The article is dated 12 September 2025. — [HellHades](https://hellhades.com/titus-blackplume-hydra-clan-boss-guide/)

### Inferences
- A skill recommending Hydra teams should first ask which heads are up this rotation, then fill counter slots: Provoke for Decay, Block Buffs for Wrath, Blight and Suffering, debuff removal or Perfect Veil for Torment, and a high-RES self-buffer for Mischief. Only then should it fill damage slots.
- Shield-scaling teams were nerfed twice before (Corpulent Cadaver, Yannica). The skill should present Titus teams as "current as of late 2025, check for changes".

### Gaps
- No readable source for specific mainstream Hydra comps beyond Titus: for example the Mithrala/Hex teams or Seer and Nekhret builds. I found no reliable source for these, so I list no names.
- Per-difficulty Hydra stats and RES requirements are in images and HellHades' Raid Stages Tool, which I couldn't read.
- Devour damage and timer values were not given.
- No source on Hydra changes in 2025-2026 beyond the Titus statement.

## 3. Chimera: mechanics, forms, ultimates, Trials, chests, team approaches

### Takeaway
Chimera is the third clan boss; HellHades calls it the "currently final" one as of 25 Nov 2025. Fights use 5 champions over 65 turns. The Chimera cycles every 5 turns between its Ultimate Form and the Ram, Lion and Viper forms, and wipes its own buffs and debuffs on each return to Ultimate. It rewards completing form-specific Trials rather than raw damage. Players get 2 keys a week, and UNM top chest needs more than 112.5M damage. It is the only source of the Mythical champion Embrys the Anomaly.

### Cited Findings
**Access and structure** (source: [ayumilove Chimera](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/))
- Requires level 33 and a clan.
- 6 difficulties, Easy to Ultra-Nightmare. Once a clan unlocks a difficulty, it stays open.
- 2 Chimera Keys per player per week. Weekly reset falls between Thursday and Friday UTC.
- 5 champions, 65 turns.
- Form order: U → R → U → L → U → V → U (U = Ultimate, R = Ram, L = Lion, V = Viper), every 5 turns, repeated twice.
- Each 28-day rotation gives Ram, Lion and Viper one shared affinity; the Ultimate Form uses a different one.

HellHades adds: Chimera is the third (and currently final) Clan Boss, has looser team-building requirements than Hydra and a unique Trial system, and is a weekly source of Starstone, Basalt and Gemstones for Relics. It is the only way to get the Mythical Embrys the Anomaly. — [HellHades Chimera boss guide, updated 25 Nov 2025](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/chimera/)

There is a rewards breakdown article dated 6 December 2024, which suggests Chimera launched in late 2024. — [HellHades Chimera rewards breakdown](https://hellhades.com/chimera-rewards-breakdown/)

**Ultimate Form**
- Baleful Torrent: AoE, Provoke, 30% Decrease SPD.
- Pincer Lash (3-turn CD): AoE that scales with debuffs on the Chimera, and grants it 30% Increase SPD.
- Consume Matter (3-turn CD): strips all enemy buffs, then attacks for more damage per buff removed.
- Transfixing Presence (passive): takes 10% less damage per enemy without buffs (up to 50%), and 50% less from Provoked enemies.
- Savage Evolution (passive): every 3 Trials completed, it gains a bonus such as ignoring Unkillable, Block Damage, DEF or Shield buffs, or gaining ATK, C.DMG or C.RATE.
- Unholy Metamorph (passive): returns it to Ultimate Form and removes all its buffs and debuffs.

— [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/)

**Ram Form (Duel)**
- Monstrous Stomp: 2 random hits that reduce turn meter and can Stun.
- Hammertail Quake: Stun plus several 2-turn debuffs.
- Maiming Stampede: converts your buffs into mirrored debuffs, then attacks.
- Lock Horns (passive): Duels the enemy with the lowest Crit Damage. Duel adds 50% damage against that champion.
- Unbreakable Focus (passive): reflects 25% of incoming damage and transfers new debuffs back to the caster.
- Ram is susceptible to Block Active Skills.

— [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-chimera-clan-boss-guide/)

**Lion Form (Hunter's Gaze)**

**Viper Form (Necrosis)**

- Deathly Contagion (passive): every ally death gives the survivors a Necrosis stack worth 5% Max HP per turn.
- Enemy of Mortality (passive): revived champions come back with less HP and turn meter, and it can deny one revive.

## 4. Doom Tower: structure, secret rooms, rotating bosses, rewards

### Takeaway
Doom Tower has 120 floors plus 12 secret rooms, in Normal and Hard. Every 10th floor is a boss, and the tower resets every 30 days on a three-rotation boss cycle. Bosses drop forge materials for exclusive artifact sets. Golden Keys open floors and Silver Keys open secret rooms and boss replays. Each boss has a single "solve" mechanic:
- Provoke against Magma Dragon's Hex.
- Cleanse against Nether Spider's poisons.
- HP Burn or Block Revive against Frost Spider.
- Max HP destruction plus shields against Scarab King.
- Fast kill against Griffin.
- A1-only play or high RES against Eternal Dragon.
- Decrease ACC against Dreadhorn.
- Affinity management against Dark Fae.

### Cited Findings
**Structure**
- 132 stages: 108 common floors, 12 boss floors and 12 secret rooms. Normal and Hard, both repeatable. Every 10th floor is a boss. Each floor has 3 rounds.
- The tower resets every 30 days, erasing progress and rankings and rotating the bosses. The release date is listed as 7 Dec 2020.

— [ayumilove Doom Tower](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)

**Keys**
- Golden Keys open a floor the first time. Silver Keys open secret rooms and replay boss floors.
- A key is spent only on a clear.
- Keys can't be bought, are capped at 10 each, and reset daily at 00:00 UTC.

— [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)

**Rotations**

| Rotation | Bosses |
|---|---|
| 1 | Magma Dragon, Nether Spider, Scarab King, Frost Spider |
| 2 | Celestial Griffin, Magma Dragon, Nether Spider, Eternal Dragon |
| 3 | Dreadhorn, Scarab King, Celestial Griffin, Dark Fae |

Source: [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/). The final boss of a rotation appears on floors 40, 80 and 120 (Dark Fae in Rotation 3). — [HellHades Dark Fae teams, Feb 2025](https://hellhades.com/the-dark-fae-heres-the-teams-they-use/)

Sources disagree on placement; one guide puts Grythion in Rotations 2 and 3. — [search summary](https://onechilledgamer.com/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-bosses-guide)

**Boss materials and the sets they forge** (source: [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/))

| Boss | Material | Set | Use |
|---|---|---|---|

**Secret rooms**
- 24 in all: 12 Normal and 12 Hard. Each needs a Silver Key, and a failed attempt doesn't consume it.
- A first clear gives 2 Silver Keys and 3 exclusive champion fragments: Epic from Normal rooms, Legendary from Hard rooms.
- Each rotation sets entry restrictions per room, for example Rare champions only, Void affinity, or 6★ champions.

— [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/). HellHades has per-room guides from 2023 (e.g. Secret Room 1 Hard, Rotation 1 and 2). — [HellHades SR1 Hard](https://hellhades.com/secret-room-1-hard-guide-rotation-1/)

**Boss mechanics and counters** (primary source: [HellHades Doom Tower Bosses](https://hellhades.com/doom-tower-bosses/))
- **Kuldath (Magma Dragon):**
  - Mechanic: Hex lets him hit one target extremely hard, ignoring that target's defences.
  - Counter: bring a Provoke champion and control the Hex.
  - Others call him the easiest DT boss, because all his abilities can be shut down. Miscreated Monster and Sandlashed Survivor are suggested controllers. — [search summary of DT boss guides](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/raid-shadow-legends/rsl-doom-tower-guide-en.html)
  - Immune to turn meter reduction (ayumilove reader comment, unconfirmed). — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)
- **Agreth (Nether Spider):**
  - Mechanic: opens by applying many poisons and detonates them if they aren't cleansed. Her 4 spiderlings put 5% Poison for 4 turns on any attacker that has no Poison on it; this can't be blocked or resisted.
  - Counter: cleansing is essential, since RES and Block Debuffs don't help.
  - Readers report she always moves first, even against a 240-speed team at stage 60 Hard. — [ayumilove comments](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)
- **Sorath (Frost Spider):**
  - Mechanic: 2 spiderlings give her ATK buffs and cleanse her debuffs, and she has a Passive Revive. Her RES is very high unless HP Burn is on her.
  - Counter: keep HP Burn on her when she goes down, or bring Lydia. Readers add Block Revive champions: Rotos, Fenax, Armiger, Lydia. — [ayumilove comments](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)
- **Borgoth (Scarab King):**
  - Mechanic: heavy damage reduction, starting at 90%. He takes full damage once his Max HP falls to 60%, and counterattacks anyone who hits him without a shield.
  - Counter: Max HP destruction skills or the Destroy (Max HP) artifact set, and keep shields up.
  - Force affinity on some Hard floors and Spirit on others. Urost can solo-farm him in about 10-15 turns at high Hard floors. — [HellHades Borgoth](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/borgoth-the-scarab-king/)
  - Reader suggestions: Metalshaper, Vergumkaar, Draconis. — [ayumilove comments](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)
- **Iragoth (Eternal Dragon):**
  - Mechanic: locks out champion abilities, and his damage grows with the number of your skills on cooldown.
  - Counter: use A1 only, or bring lots of RES. Don't ignore his summoned allies, or he gains Eternal Rage. — [search summary](https://hellhades.com/doom-tower-introducing-grythion-iragoth/)
- **Grythion (Celestial Griffin):**
  - Mechanic: enrage. Sky Bond stacks raise his ATK, C.RATE, C.DMG and SPD, and he eventually gains Ignore Shield, Block Damage and Unkillable.
  - Counter: a fast-kill team.
- **Bommal (Dreadhorn):**
  - Mechanic: spawns DreadBomb allies that deal damage based on your HP and puts Bomb debuffs on you. His base stats are very high, and he also gates several Cursed City stages.
  - Counter: resisting the bombs is nearly impossible without Decrease ACC.
  - HellHades calls him one of the hardest DT bosses (page updated 21 May 2024). — [HellHades Dreadhorn](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/bommal-the-dreadhorn/)
- **Astranyx (Dark Fae):**
  - Mechanic: mirrors your champions, so stronger teams face stronger mirrors. The mirrors use Ally Attack, and her effects depend on the affinity she attacks and how many of that affinity are targeted. Void affinity on Hard.
  - Suggested Hard stats: SPD 250+, ACC 355+, RES 375+.
  - HellHades' team: Holsring (turn meter control), Hegemon (damage that clears the mirrors), Gnut and Acrizia (Max HP damage and debuffs). It clears Hard 120 in 39 turns. — [HellHades Dark Fae](https://hellhades.com/the-dark-fae-heres-the-teams-they-use/)

**Champions for floors and Hard mode**
- ayumilove ranks floor champions by star tier. Readers suggest Warmaiden for floors, and free options for Hard: Mithrala, Artak, Sun Wukong, Visix. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)
- Against Celestial Griffin and Eternal Dragon, the ayumilove author suggests a high-RES team with heavy damage plus ACC and RES debuffers. — [ayumilove](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-doom-tower-guide/)

### Inferences
- A skill can map each DT boss to one must-have tool:
  - Magma Dragon: Provoke.
  - Nether Spider: an AoE cleanser.
  - Frost Spider: HP Burn or Block Revive.
  - Scarab King: Destroy Max HP plus Shields.
  - Celestial Griffin: burst damage.
  - Eternal Dragon: A1-reliant or high-RES team.
  - Dreadhorn: Decrease ACC.
  - Dark Fae: mirror-aware team that tolerates or controls Ally Attack.
- Bosses also drop the forge materials for the DT-exclusive sets, so farming priority follows which set the player wants. For example, Untouchable from Scarab King for anti-debuff builds, and Fortitude from Dreadhorn for RES supports.

### Gaps
- The user's list included Silver (currency) rewards and DT sets (Stone Skin, Feral, Pinpoint and others). The sources I read don't tie those sets to Doom Tower, so I can't confirm whether Doom Tower grants them, and I found no per-floor reward table.
- Floor numbers for each boss per rotation, and whether Plarium has added new DT bosses since 2024, are unconfirmed. ayumilove lists only these 8 bosses and 3 rotations, with a warning to verify in game.
- Detailed Hard stat requirements per boss, except Dark Fae.

Across these sources, the cheapest reliable path for CB is a Maneater-based unkillable team (with Pain Keeper or Demytha) or a free-to-play poison team built on Kael. Hydra currently revolves around Titus with Wixwell or Brogni and buff extenders.

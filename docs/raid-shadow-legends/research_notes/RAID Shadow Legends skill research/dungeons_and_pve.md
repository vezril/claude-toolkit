## Q1. Keep dungeons: Dragon's Lair, Ice Golem's Peak, Fire Knight's Castle, Spider's Den, Minotaur's Labyrinth

### Takeaway
All four classic artifact dungeons (Dragon, Ice Golem, Fire Knight, Spider) have 25 Normal stages and a Hard mode. Hard mode arrived in Patch 6.51.1 (March 9, 2023) and fights a "Tainted" version of the boss that can drop Mythical-rarity artifacts. From stage 21 up, every classic boss gains anti-cheese passives: Max-HP damage is capped at 10% of the boss's max HP, and Turn Meter reduction is halved. Minotaur has 15 stages and drops Mastery Scrolls. Since Aug 6, 2025 it supports Super Raids, and since Apr 1, 2026 unused scrolls go to other champions.

**Dragon's Lair — Hellrazor (Normal) / Tainted Hellrazor (Hard)**
- Mechanics: *Swipe* hits all enemies and applies −50% ATK for 2 turns. *Wall of Fire* (3-turn cooldown) is an AoE that applies two 5% Poisons and a 25% Weaken. *Inhale* (3-turn cooldown) empties his own Turn Meter and arms *Scorch*, an AoE that stuns for 1 turn. You stop Scorch by dealing enough damage to clear the purple HP bar before his next turn. He is immune to Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Fear and Turn Meter reduction, so you can't slow him. At stages 21–25 the Max-HP damage cap and halved Turn Meter reduction apply. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- An older ayumilove note: the Decrease Attack debuff is what stops weak teams from clearing the purple bar. — [ayumilove (search snippet)](https://ayumilove.net/?p=48226)
- Tainted Hellrazor: *Mephitic Fire* applies four 5% Poisons and a Weaken, and gives him +50% ATK. *Draconic Gaze* empties every enemy Turn Meter and arms *Toxic Incineration*, which deals pure damage, applies an unresistable Stun and instantly detonates Poisons. *Awakened Weakness* means he deals less damage to awakened champions and takes more from them. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- Strategy: Decrease ATK, Weaken, and several heavy nukers to break the purple bar. A support with Block Debuff and cleanse. About 300 Resistance cited for stage 20. Revive and Ally Protection for safety. For speed farms, tune turn order as ATK buffer, then Decrease DEF debuffer, then nuker (sub-2-minute runs). For Hard: Decrease DEF + Weaken + Decrease ATK, Increase DEF, Shield, Bolster or Defiant sets, and a cleanser or Block Debuff champion are essential. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- Affinities: Magic 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 20, 22; Force 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 21, 25; Spirit 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 24; Void 4, 8, 12, 16, 23. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- Drop sets: Accuracy, Speed, Lifesteal, Destroy, Toxic, Frost, Daze, Avenging, Stalwart. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- HellHades rates Dragon as Easy and as the early/mid-game source of the Speed set. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)
- Mythical 5★ champions ayumilove lists highly: Androc, Ashnar Dragonsoul, Blood Marchioness Mina, Embrys, Heinrik Demondoom, Joan the Luminant, Kurosa, Night Queen Krixia, Siegfrund, Starsage Galathir, The Calamitus, Theodosia. — [ayumilove Dragon](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-dragons-lair/)
- Fren'zi is suggested for both Fire Knight and Dragon because her defense-based kit lasts through long fights. — [BlueStacks (search snippet)](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/raid-shadow-legends/rsl-best-team-for-hard-dungeon-en.html)

**Ice Golem's Peak — Klyssus / Tainted Klyssus**
- Mechanics: *Frost Nova* is an AoE. *Numbing Chill* (4-turn cooldown) applies −50% ACC for 2 turns. The passive *Frigid Vengeance* triggers at 80/60/45/30/15% HP: it revives dead minions at full HP and gains a Freeze chance for each living ally. Two minions apply Heal Reduction and Decrease DEF. The boss has the usual CC immunities, plus the stage 21–25 caps (Max-HP damage at 10%, Turn Meter reduction halved). — [ayumilove Ice Golem](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-ice-golems-peak/)
- Tainted Klyssus: *Debilitating Frost* cuts enemy Turn Meters by 30% and strips debuffs off the boss. *Winter's Power* makes it immune to Poison, makes it take 10% more HP Burn damage, and lets HP Burn or Smite shrink minion max HP. — [ayumilove Ice Golem](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-ice-golems-peak/)
- Strategies: (1) Block Revive with enough damage to one-shot the minions. (2) Poison or HP Burn, which damage the boss on its own turn without hitting the HP thresholds yourself. (3) Sustain plus AoE heal, revive, cleanse and CC on the minions. (4) Speed clears under 3 minutes with Decrease DEF, ATK/DEF buffs and Increase SPD. Hard mode: HP Burn champions (Ninja and Walking Tomb Dreng are named), Brimstone Blessing, Bolster or Defiant sets, Increase DEF, Shield and Decrease ATK. — [ayumilove Ice Golem](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-ice-golems-peak/)
- Affinities: Spirit 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 20, 24; Magic 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 21, 25; Force 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23; Void 4, 8, 12, 16, 22. — [ayumilove Ice Golem](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-ice-golems-peak/)
- Drop sets: Life, Offense, Defense, Critical Rate, Resistance, Retaliation, Reflex, Cursed, Taunting. — [ayumilove Ice Golem](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-ice-golems-peak/)
- HellHades rates Ice Golem as Hard: it has some of the best sets, but a consistent team is hard to build. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)
- Teumesia has a build aimed at soloing Spider and Ice Golem. — [ayumilove Teumesia](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-teumesia-skill-mastery-equip-guide/)

**Fire Knight's Castle — Fyro / Tainted Fyro**
- Mechanics: Fyro starts the fight with a *Divine Shield* that absorbs 80% of damage and makes him debuff-immune. The shield takes 5 hits to break on stages 1–6, 7 on stages 7–9, 10 on stages 10–20 and 12 on stages 21–25, and it regenerates every turn. If the shield is still up when his turn starts, he heals and hits the whole team, cutting max HP by up to 40% and possibly one-shotting fragile champions. Immunities: Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Block Skills, Fear, True Fear, HP exchange/balance and cooldown increase. At stages 21–25, Turn Meter reduction is halved. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- Tainted Fyro: each hit on the shield destroys 1% of the attacker's max HP. His attacks can apply −30% SPD and −60% DEF. Freeze is removed instantly, and each removal drops his Turn Meter by 15%. He is also immune to Petrification and Turn Meter decrease. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- Archetypes: three or more multi-hit A1 skills (about 10 hits per round to break the shield). Increase SPD and Increase Turn Meter. Counterattack or Reflect Damage for slower teams. CC on the minions. Block Cooldown and Decrease Turn Meter on Fyro. Hard: Heal Reduction, Weaken and Decrease DEF after the shield breaks, Decrease SPD and Freeze to stop the re-shield, and Brimstone Blessing for the waves. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- Linked Hard 4 teams: Padraig / Lydia / Criodan / Neldor / Yakarl, and Padraig / Lydia / Neldor / Rathalos / Gnut. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- Affinities: Force 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 20, 24; Spirit 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 21, 25; Magic 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 22; Void 4, 8, 12, 16, 23. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- Drop sets: Fury, Curing, Immunity, Shield, Critical Damage, Frenzy, Regeneration, Stun, Savage. — [ayumilove Fire Knight](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-fire-knights-castle/)
- HellHades rates Fire Knight as Very Hard and notes it is the source of Savage. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)

**Spider's Den — Skavag / Tainted Skavag**
- Mechanics: Skavag summons a Spiderling whenever one of your champions gets an extra turn or starts a turn, up to 10 per battle. Spiderlings apply Poison (5% of max HP per stack), which can kill even high-HP tanks. She eats her Spiderlings to heal and permanently gains ATK, so long fights get worse. She is CC-immune. At stages 21–25 she resists Turn Meter effects and caps Max-HP damage. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/), [ayumilove (snippet)](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- Stat rules of thumb: Accuracy of about Resist + 25, or about 11 × stage, capped at 225. Resistance of about boss Accuracy + 100, capped at 300. Stage 20 has 200 Accuracy and 200 Resistance. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- Stage strategy: on stages 1–14, an AoE nuke kills the Spiderlings before they act. On 15–20, use Enemy Max HP nukes, Poison explosions or HP Burn. On 21–25, Max-HP damage is capped, so AoE HP Burn is recommended. Supporting tools: Turn Meter control, AoE Shield, Stun or Freeze, a bait tank, and cooldown reset. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- Named champions by role. Nukers: Coldheart, Royal Guard, Husk, Karam, Zavia, Elenaril. Starters: Kael, Athel, Elhain, Galek. Decrease DEF/Weaken: Warmaiden, Armina, Stag Knight. Poison: Taurus, Bad-el-Kazar, Tomb Lord. Turn Meter: Lyssandra, Jintoro, Armiger. Cooldown reset: Renegade, Prince Kymar. Survival: Miscreated Monster, Valkyrie, Sir Nicholas, Helior, Crypt-King Graal, Scyl, Achak. Bait: Sinesha, Zephyr Sniper, Basilisk. Tainted Skavag: Sinesha, Skullcrown, Acrizia. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- Tainted Skavag: Tainted Spiderlings. She is immune to HP Burn, Decrease SPD and Turn Meter reduction. Each Tainted Spiderling that dies deals 3% of her max HP to her. Awakening Weakness scales her damage with awakening level. Recommended: AoE damage with Decrease DEF and Weaken. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- Drops: accessories (Ring, Amulet, Banner) across 14 factions, plus Mythical artifacts in Hard mode. — [ayumilove Spider](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-spiders-den/)
- HellHades rates Spider as Easy, the only accessory source, and important for Clan vs Clan. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)

**Minotaur's Labyrinth**
- Mechanics: *Raging Bull* gives the Minotaur Rage (+400% damage) for 3 turns. When it ends he is Dazed for 1 turn and takes +200% damage, then the cycle repeats. These effects can't be cleansed. *Swift Doom* (2-turn cooldown) is an AoE that places an unresistable Hex on 3 random targets for 5 turns. *Tremor Stomp* is an AoE that deals double damage to Hexed targets. Immunities: Stun, Freeze, Sleep, Provoke, Block Active Skills, Fear, True Fear, HP exchange/balance and cooldown increase. Counter: a cleanser for Hex, or Block Debuff. — [ayumilove Minotaur](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-minotaurs-labyrinth/)
- Stages: 15. Affinities: Spirit 1, 5, 9, 13; Magic 2, 6, 10, 14; Void 3, 7, 11, 15; Force 4, 8, 12. Drops: Basic, Advanced and Divine Mastery Scrolls. — [ayumilove Minotaur](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-minotaurs-labyrinth/)
- HellHades rates Minotaur as Easy; it holds the scrolls needed for 6★ champion masteries. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)
- V10.60 (2025-08-06): time-limited Super Raids in Minotaur, a MAX indicator, and auto-stop when champions hit max Scrolls. V11.40 (2026-04-01): Scroll distribution improved so unused Scrolls go to other champions. V11.65 (2026-07-02): a filter to hide champions with all Mastery Scrolls. — [ayumilove 2025](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2025/), [ayumilove 2026](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2026/)

### Inferences
- Progression order for the skill to recommend: Dragon (Speed set, Easy) and Spider (accessories, Easy) first. Then Minotaur once champions approach 6★ masteries. Then Fire Knight and Ice Golem (Very Hard / Hard; Savage, Retaliation, Reflex, Cursed and so on). Hard modes after a team reliably auto-clears stage 25. This follows from HellHades' difficulty ratings and the drop lists above.
- Universal stat ceilings for stage 20–25 farming: Accuracy about 225 for Spider (cap); Resistance about 300 for Dragon 20 and Spider. Hard modes and stages 21–25 punish Max-HP nukers (10% cap) and Turn Meter control (halved), which favors HP Burn, Poison and raw damage with Decrease DEF + Weaken.
- Hard modes all hinge on Awakening (Tainted Hellrazor and Tainted Skavag scale damage by awakening level), so the skill should treat awakened champions (via Iron Twins Soul Coins) as a prerequisite for Hard 7–10.
- **[background knowledge, unverified]** Hard mode has 10 stages for each of the four dungeons. Popular speed-farm patterns: Spider 20/25 with Coldheart + Skullcrown-type Decrease DEF/Weaken setups, or Teumesia / Venomage solos. Dragon 25 with HP-Burn plus nuker (for example Ninja, Duchess Lilitu). Fire Knight with multi-hitters (Fenax, Martyr, Ninja). Ice Golem with Block Revive + Poison/HP Burn (Venomage, Rhazin). Common budget anchors: Kael (Poison, campaign starter), Coldheart (Max-HP nuke), Armina (Decrease DEF + Weaken), High Khatun (Increase SPD), Scyl (team sustain).

## Q3. Newer dungeons: Iron Twins Fortress, Sand Devil's Necropolis, Phantom Shogun's Grove, and 2024–2026 additions

### Takeaway
There are three "resource" dungeons. Iron Twins (15 stages, affinity rotates daily) drops Soul Coins and Essence for Awakening. Sand Devil (released Dec 2022, 25 stages) drops Artifact Oil for Artifact Ascension. Phantom Shogun (25 stages) drops Accessory Extract and Chaos Powder for Accessory Ascension. No new permanent boss dungeon of this kind was found for 2025–2026. The new permanent PvE modes are **Grim Forest** (V11.00, 2025-12-10) and apparently **Champion Proving Grounds** (2026; see Q6). "Kuldath" is a Doom Tower boss (Magma Dragon), not a dungeon.

### Cited Findings

**Iron Twins Fortress**
- Daily reset, and the affinity changes every day. 15 stages; farm the highest one you can clear. Drops: Mortal Soul Coin, Immortal Soul Essence, Eternal Soul Essence. — [ayumilove Iron Twins](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-for-iron-twins-fortress/)
- Soul Coins are used to awaken champions. — [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/) (rated Moderate to Hard)
- Mechanics: *Ironbrand* does nothing on its own but powers the *Doomsday Machine* nuke, which gets stronger the longer Ironbrand stays on; it can't be blocked or removed but can be resisted. *Fires of Insanity* and *Ruinous Swath* apply or extend Ironbrand based on the target's DEF or ATK. A *Growth* effect raises the twins' DEF, with more if your affinities don't match. *Retaliatory Launch* at 80/60/40% HP hits all enemies and cleanses the twins. Each buff on them fills their Turn Meter 10%, and Decrease SPD prevents this. They are immune to Turn Meter reduction, CC, cooldown blocks and HP swaps. Poison is 90% less effective. Multi-hit skills lose effectiveness on each hit (use single-hit nukes). HP Burn is reduced but usable. — [ayumilove Iron Twins](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-for-iron-twins-fortress/)
- Stat requirements. Stages 1–6: SPD 150+, ACC 150+, RES 205+, HP 25K / DEF 2400. Stages 7–12: SPD 220+, ACC 275+, RES 355+, 40K / 2800. Stages 13–15: SPD 250+, ACC 375+, RES 505+, 50K / 3500. The Accuracy and Resistance requirements roughly drop by a third or a half with 50% Increase ACC or Decrease ACC. — [ayumilove Iron Twins](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-for-iron-twins-fortress/)
- Example team: Iron Twins 15 (Void) with Mithrala, Duchess, Krisk, Champfort and Geomancer. — [ayumilove Iron Twins](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-for-iron-twins-fortress/)
- Common approaches: Geomancer as the core damage dealer plus a Decrease SPD champion. A Block Damage, full-auto team is documented by DeadwoodJedi. — [Inteleria (snippet)](https://www.inteleria.com/how-to-beat-iron-twins-in-raid-and-best-champions/), [DeadwoodJedi: Full Auto Block Damage Iron Twins](https://deadwoodjedi.com/full-auto-block-damage-iron-twins-fortress/)

**Sand Devil's Necropolis (Al-Naemeh)**
- Released December 2022 as an end-game dungeon for farming Oil to ascend artifacts and accessories. Duo strategies for stage 25: Godseeker Aniri + Ninja, and Godseeker Aniri + Selinia. — [ayumilove Sand Devil (snippet)](https://ayumilove.net/?p=77355); [HellHades: New Dungeon! Artifact Ascension! (Dec 1, 2022)](https://hellhades.com/new-dungeon-artifact-ascension-raid-shadow-legends/)
- Unlocks at account level 38. Rated Very Hard ("don't wake the sleeping giant"). The HellHades boss page was last updated June 10, 2024, for the Patch 8.60 boss changes, which means Sand Devil was rebalanced in June 2024. — [HellHades Al-Naemeh boss guide](https://hellhades.com/raid/boss-guide/al-naemeh-the-sand-devil/), [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)
- Farm-stage advice: stage 7 is the most efficient for Lesser Oil, stage 24 for Greater Oil and stage 25 for Superior Oil. Stage 25 speed teams that kill in 6 turns exist. Sand Devil has Super Raids. — [HellHades (snippet)](https://hellhades.com/al-naemeh-the-sand-devil-guide/), [HH Gaming's Sand Devil 25 speed teams, Nov 5, 2024](https://hellhades.com/hh-gamings-sand-devil-25-speed-teams/), [HellHades: Sand Devil gets Super Raids](https://hellhades.com/sand-devil-gets-super-raids/)
- Suggested team shape: 1 Sleep champion + 1–2 Max-HP damage dealers + 1 debuffer + 1 healer or reviver. — [Inteleria (snippet)](https://www.inteleria.com/how-to-beat-sand-devil-in-raid-and-best-champions-team/)
- V10.60.5 (2025-08-21): the Advanced Quest requirement for Sand Devil and Phantom Shogun dropped from 5 clears to 3. — [ayumilove Patch Notes 2025](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2025/)
- An Oct 30, 2026 HellHades piece on Sand Devil mechanics exists, but it wasn't read. — [HellHades: Understanding the Sand Devil's mechanics](https://hellhades.com/understanding-the-sand-devils-mechanics-in-raid-shadow-legends)

**Phantom Shogun's Grove (Akumori)**
- The main source of Extract for Accessory Ascension (unlocks at level 38, works like Artifact Ascension). Rated Hard. — [ayumilove Artifact & Accessory guide (snippet)](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-artifact-and-accessory-guide/), [HellHades dungeon guides](https://hellhades.com/raid/dungeon-guides/)
- Drops: Lesser Extract on stages 1–25 (ascension levels 1–2), Greater Extract on stages 10–25 (levels 3–4), Superior Extract on stages 17–25 (levels 5–6), plus Chaos Powder. — [ayumilove Phantom Shogun](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-for-phantom-shogun-grove/)

## Q4. Faction Wars

### Takeaway
Faction Wars has one crypt per faction (21 at the time of writing; per-faction keys, stars out of 3). Each crypt has 21 stages, with Crypt Keeper bosses at stages 7, 14 and 21. Rewards: Glyphs, forge materials (Resilience / Perception / Deflection / Swift Parry sets), **Lydia the Deathsiren** for full 3★ Normal, and **Polara Fireheart** (Mythical) for full Hard. Hard Mode launched in V10.45 (2025-05-13) and was rebalanced in V11.80 (2026-10-08: lower enemy stats on stages 1–21) and V11.90 (late Oct 2026: relaxed challenge conditions).

### Cited Findings
- Mode: use champions of one faction to clear that faction's Crypt. Rewards are Glyphs (artifact substats) and forge materials for Resilience, Perception, Deflection and Swift Parry. Stages are cleared in order. Stars: 1★ for a clear, 2★ with at least 3 champions alive, 3★ with all 5 alive. Star totals unlock one-time rewards. Full 3★ Normal gives Lydia the Deathsiren. Advanced Quests give bonus FW keys for 5 Tag Team Arena battles. — [ayumilove Faction Wars](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-faction-wars/)
- Hard Mode unlocks after Normal and has stronger Crypt Keepers and minions, higher-rarity Glyphs and Faction-Wars-specific crafting materials. Full Hard completion gives Polara Fireheart (Mythical). — [ayumilove Faction Wars](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-faction-wars/), [LDPlayer FW Hard guide (snippet)](https://www.ldplayer.net/blog/raid-shadow-legends-faction-wars-hard-mode-guide.html)
- V10.45 (2025-05-13) introduced Faction Wars Hard Mode with tougher stages, new Crypt Keepers, Spiteful Remains relic materials and 8 new Relics. The FW relic group has 8 relics: 2 Rare, 2 Epic, 2 Legendary, 2 Mythical. First clears of Hard stages 7, 14 and 21 give one-time rewards. — [ayumilove Patch Notes 2025](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-patch-notes-2025/), [Plarium forum: Update Highlights 10.45.0](https://forum.plarium.com/raid-shadow-legends/843_news/43286_update-highlights-10-45-0), [HellHades: FW Hard what you need to know (May 9, 2025)](https://hellhades.com/faction-wars-hard-mode-what-you-need-to-know/)

- There are five Crypt Keeper boss types, with counters:
  - **Red / Dueler:** stuns everyone except one target with unresistable Stun and True Fear. Counter with Block Debuff or cleanse, a healer and Ally Protection, and favor durability over damage.
  - **Purple / Mirrorer:** Fear, buff steal, debuff transfer, and an HP swap every 12 turns. Kill the two minions first, run high Resistance, and burst the boss before the swap.
  - **Blue / Protector:** minions hit based on the target's HP. CC the minions but don't kill them, because killing them gives the boss Block Damage.
  - **Yellow / Disruptor:** Provoke, True Fear and a Freeze passive. Counter with Block Debuff, cleanse or high Resistance.
  - **Green / Incinerator:** Poison, Turn Meter reduction and HP Burn when hit. Counter with Block Debuff, cleanse and a strong healer, and kill the minions first.

Blue: Banner Lords, Sacred Order. Yellow: High Elves, Barbarians, Demonspawn. — [ayumilove Faction Wars](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-champion-ranking-in-faction-wars/)

## Q5. Campaign farming, XP, food and Silver

### Takeaway
Brutal 12-3 is still the standard farm stage for both XP and Silver (shields sell well). Energy cost per run: Normal 4, Hard 6, Brutal 8, Nightmare 16. XP is split evenly among the champions in a run, so run a single strong farmer plus up to three pieces of food. Since 2025, Campaign Super Raids (V10.20), Background Multi-Battle (V10.30) and the Platinum/Diamond Raid Cards (V11.40) have changed the economics.

### Cited Findings
- Brutal 12-3 balances energy and XP; Nightmare gives more XP but takes longer. Energy per battle: Normal 4, Hard 6, Brutal 8, Nightmare 16. XP is split equally among the champions in the run, and XP going to maxed champions is wasted. — [ayumilove Campaign XP & Silver guide (updated Sep 13, 2026)](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-campaign-xp-silver-guide/)

- XP multipliers: an XP Boost gives +100% (multiplicative). The Raid Card gives +20% Silver and XP from Campaign (multiplicative). The Forge Pass Raid Boost gives up to +50% (additive). — [ayumilove Campaign XP guide](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-campaign-xp-silver-guide/)
- Brutal 12-3 has the best balance of Silver, XP and energy. If you can't clear it, farm stage 3 of the highest chapter you can (10-3, 11-3). Build your first 6★ 60 before serious Brutal 12-3 farming. A clear time of 15 seconds or less is "great". Shields drop at 12-3 and sell for the most Silver. — [Inteleria / HellHades snippets](https://www.inteleria.com/how-to-farm-in-raid-shadow-legends-the-definitive-efficiency-guide/), [HellHades: best place to level](https://hellhades.com/where-is-the-best-place-to-level-your-champions-in-raid-shadow-legends/), [HellHades: What makes an effective 12-3 Brutal Campaign Farmer?](https://hellhades.com/what-makes-an-effective-12-3-brutal-campaign-farmer/comment-page-1/)

## Q6. Cursed City, Grim Forest, Champion Proving Grounds, events

### Takeaway
**Cursed City (Sintranos)** has existed since Patch 8.0 (2024). It is a non-linear 101-floor map per difficulty (Normal and Hard), unlocks at account level 52, rotates monthly with per-stage champion restrictions, and ends with **Amius the Lunar Archon**, who rewards the Mythical champion Amius. **Grim Forest** (V11.00, 2025-12-10, level 55) is a roguelite deck mode with a 30-day reset and final boss Leshun; it rewards Anaxia the Reborn (Mythical), the Mercurial set and Bloodbriar relics. **Champion Proving Grounds** appears to be a permanent 27-stage, 28-day rotating dungeon from 2026 that drops Anomalous Accessories.
